### AI & I: A $10B Hedge Fund’s AI Playbook (Best of the Pod)

**The Takeaway:** Walleye CEO/CIO Will is forcing a ~$10B AUM hedge fund to treat AI like the internet in 1995: use it now, lead from the front, and treat "wait until it's perfect" as an excuse that leaves money on the table.

**要点：** 管理约 $10B AUM 的对冲基金 Walleye 的 CEO/CIO Will，把 AI 当成 1995 年的互联网：现在就用、一把手带头，并把“等它完美再说”视为把钱留在桌上的借口。

Will rarely does press; he says he showed up because he feels responsible to three audiences: Walleye's ~400 people, future hires who need to see how serious the firm is, and the broader ecosystem of builders selling into funds like his. An engineer by training (Princeton, Oxford math PhD, early career writing code for algorithmic strategies), he argues hedge funds have used advanced statistics and AI in quant trading for years; what changed is that LLMs made powerful tools accessible to non-technical people, especially on unstructured data. Two years ago Walleye started an internal AI program; the mandate is firm-wide proficiency, not a future slide deck.

Will 很少接受媒体访问；他说现身是因为要对三类人负责：Walleye 约 400 名员工、需要看到公司有多认真的未来招聘对象，以及卖给这类基金的生态建设者。他本人是工程师出身（Princeton、Oxford 数学博士，职业生涯早期为算法策略写代码），认为对冲基金在量化交易里用高级统计乃至 AI 已有多年；真正变了的是 LLM 让强工具对非技术人也可用，尤其是在处理非结构化数据时。两年前 Walleye 就启动了内部 AI 项目；目标是全员具备基线能力，而不是一张“未来规划”幻灯片。

His AI-at-Walleye memo opens with the line that sticks: "Using ChatGPT is not cheating. That's a non applicable idea from academia. I use ChatGPT to write this email. You should be using it too and be proud of it." He equates skipping these tools with refusing the internet in 1995 because it was imperfect, and says a hedge fund should be ashamed to leave money on the table by ignoring leverage that makes people faster and smarter. The anxiety he hears is job obsolescence; his answer is operating leverage. A long/short picker who cannot build a model in Excel is already obsolete; soon, people and firms without AI tool fluency will be in the same spot. Walleye deliberately hires mid-career people at an inflection point: competent enough to be dangerous, still hungry enough to learn.

他写给全公司的 AI memo 开头就是那句最刺眼的话：“Using ChatGPT is not cheating. That's a non applicable idea from academia. I use ChatGPT to write this email. You should be using it too and be proud of it.”他把不用这些工具比作 1995 年因为互联网不完美而拒绝上网，并说作为对冲基金，忽略能让人更快、更聪明的杠杆，等于把钱留在桌上，应当感到羞愧。他听到的焦虑是岗位被替代；他的回答是 operating leverage。不会用 Excel 建公司模型的多空选股人已经过时；很快，不会用 AI 工具、或所在公司给不了工具杠杆的人，也会落到同一位置。Walleye 刻意招处在职业拐点的中生代：已经够能打，但仍有饥渴去学新技能。

Concrete gains sit in two buckets. Quant equity has used nonlinear stats and AI-ish models for years; LLMs now scale unstructured-data / sentiment-style signals into the money-making stack. Across accounting, finance, compliance, and legal, he wants people using ChatGPT or other LLMs for analysis and writing rather than waiting for perfection. He even runs imperfect demos in front of about a third of the company on purpose, so the culture flips from "it failed once, ignore it" to "direction of travel is positive, keep going." On writing specifically, he wants humans spending time on concepts, not "tying your shoes" sentence stitching; editing for cleverness and voice matters less once the model handles the glue.

具体收益分两块。量化股票业务多年前就在用非线性统计和类 AI 模型；LLM 则把非结构化数据 / 情绪类信号规模化进赚钱引擎。在会计、财务、合规、法务等部门，他希望大家用 ChatGPT 或其他 LLM 做分析与写作，而不是干等完美。他甚至故意在约三分之一公司同事面前跑会翻车的 demo，好把文化从“失败一次就无视”翻成“方向是对的，继续用”。具体到写作，他要人把时间花在概念上，而不是“系鞋带”式的句子拼接；一旦模型能处理连接工作，为了机灵措辞和风格抠细节的编辑就没那么重要了。

The deeper frame is responsibility. If AI disrupts Walleye, he says that is on him. Leaders who can act must lead from the front; waiting for tools to be flawless is how firms fall behind while peers compound operating leverage. The cowboy / barbed-wire metaphor he likes is the same frontier tension as tech: explorers push without rules, then civilization institutionalizes; the winners in transitions are outfits that can push the frontier with real resources, not single-action rifles against machine guns.

更深的框架是责任。如果 AI 颠覆了 Walleye，他说那是他的问题。有能力行动的领导者必须带头；等工具完美才动手，等于看着同行用 operating leverage 复利，自己掉队。他爱用的牛仔 / 铁丝网隐喻，也是技术前沿的同一张力：开拓者先在无规则处推进，随后文明制度化；转型期的赢家，是那些既有 frontier 心态、又有真实资源去推进的组织，而不是拿单发步枪去对抗机关枪。
