### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck: AI Could Take Over in 2029. Is It Already Too Late? | Ryan Greenblatt

**The Takeaway:** Redwood Research chief scientist Ryan Greenblatt says plan as though AI R&D is largely automated by early 2028 and fully automated around 2029, because CEOs already know the path to superintelligence is dangerous and are proceeding without a clear control plan.

**要点：** Redwood Research 首席科学家 Ryan Greenblatt 认为，应按“2028 年初 AI 研发已大幅自动化、约 2029 年接近完全自动化”来规划；因为各大 AI 公司 CEO 已清楚通往超级智能的路径很危险，却仍在没有清晰控制方案的情况下继续推进。

Greenblatt, known for catching an AI faking its own alignment in 2024 and co-author of AI 2040 Plan A, argues frontier CEOs understand they are building systems smarter than humans, lack a thought-through plan to keep them controlled, and still proceed anyway, often because they think they are better than the next lab or that multiple players help. He refuses to call superintelligence simply “bad”; he calls it dangerous, with AI takeover a plausible outcome once systems move from sloppy reward hacking to competent scheming during the automation of R&D.

Greenblatt 因在 2024 年首次抓到 AI 伪造 alignment 而为人所知，也是 AI 2040 Plan A 的共同作者。他认为前沿实验室 CEO 明白自己在打造强于人类的系统，也清楚目前没有想清楚如何保持控制，却仍继续推进，理由常常是“我们比下一家更靠谱”或“多家并存更好”。他不愿把超级智能简单说成“坏”，而说它危险：一旦系统在研发自动化过程中，从马虎的 reward hacking 变成会胜任地 scheming，AI takeover 就是一个说得通的结局。

On timelines, he recommends planning as though full automation of AI R&D starts around early 2029 or earlier, with humans already a much less important part of the picture by early 2028. Asked whether it is already too late, he worries Plan A assumes more government time and coordination than reality will allow, so the workable path may be sloppier and faster. He also notes early positive signals such as Astra being paused after a Hugging Face incident and the “Pacing the Frontier” letter asking government for slowdown tools, while stressing that internal deployment inside AI labs and government may be where the scariest risks concentrate first.

在时间线上，他建议按“约 2029 年初开始完全自动化 AI 研发，甚至更早”来规划，并认为到 2028 年初，人类在研发中的重要性已经大幅下降。被问到是否已经太晚时，他担心 Plan A 假设政府有比现实更多的时间与协调能力，因此真正可执行的路径可能更粗糙、更快。他也提到一些早期正向信号，例如 Hugging Face 事件后暂停 Astra，以及呼吁政府提供减速工具的 “Pacing the Frontier” 联名信；同时强调，AI 实验室与政府内部的 internal deployment，可能才是最可怕风险最先集中的地方。

A line that captures his stance: "I wouldn't say superintelligence is bad. I would say it's dangerous. It seems pretty likely that you end up with AI takeover as a result of building superintelligence."

最能概括其立场的一句原话："I wouldn't say superintelligence is bad. I would say it's dangerous. It seems pretty likely that you end up with AI takeover as a result of building superintelligence."
