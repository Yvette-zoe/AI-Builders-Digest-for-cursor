### AI & I by Every: How a Professional Writer Writes With AI

**The Takeaway:** Compound Writing treats writing like Compound Engineering: every piece of feedback should feed back into the system so the next draft starts smarter, turning AI writing from a chat window into a reusable gym for taste and craft.

**要点：** Compound Writing 把写作做成 Compound Engineering 那一套：每条反馈都该回流进系统，让下一稿起点更高，把 AI 写作从聊天窗变成可复用的写作健身房。

Every staff writer Katie Parrott, interviewed by Natalia Quintero (Every head of AI consulting), with Dan Shipper introducing, describes Compound Writing as a fork of Kieran Klaassen's Compound Engineering plus Dan's agent-native architecture, remapped onto brainstorm → plan → draft → review. "Compounding is the idea that every piece of feedback that you give to AI should feed back into the system to improve the next output." She no longer lives in a bare chat or project pane; she works in the Claude or ChatGPT desktop app inside a recipe that already knows her drafting process, style guides, and example pieces, then drops in fresh research for each article.

Her path into AI writing started earlier than the plugin: after a layoff she used ChatGPT as a cheap career coach, then learned that upfront context engineering (audience, pain points, product map, competitors) creates rails so the model can run fast. Diction and tone come later; foundational context comes first. "Clarity, it turns out, doesn't arrive gift wrapped from a digital assistant...
