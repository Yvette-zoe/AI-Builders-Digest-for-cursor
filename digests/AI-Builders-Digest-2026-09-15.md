**OpenAI CEO Sam Altman** says the world needs confidence that US frontier labs will act responsibly as capability progress steepens, welcomes a federal safety framework, and argues labs should not wait for antitrust exemptions or new laws to start. He frames two failure modes to avoid: losing control of the future to AI (stay on "Team Humanity"; keep alignment/safety ahead of capabilities), and extreme concentration of power in one person, company, country, or lab. On "pacing," he stresses it does not mean stopping: progress will continue, but should be slower than the maximum possible because tools like pre-run safety cases and monitoring have real costs; OpenAI now writes explicit safety cases before frontier RL runs expected to jump capability. He wants shared industry standards on misalignment, monitoring, and safety, with government help mainly for international coordination.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 称，随着能力爬升变陡，世界需要确信美国前沿实验室会负责任地行动；他欢迎联邦层面的安全框架，并主张不必等反垄断豁免或新立法才动手。他强调要避免两条失败路径：把未来的控制权丢给 AI（站在 "Team Humanity"，让 alignment/safety 始终领先能力），以及权力过度集中在个人、公司、国家或单一实验室。关于 "pacing"，他强调不是停止：进步会继续，但应慢于理论最快速度，因为预先 safety case 与监控等工具有真实成本；OpenAI 现在会在预计大幅提升能力的 frontier RL runs 之前写明 safety case。他希望行业在 misalignment、监控与安全上形成共享标准，政府主要帮忙做国际协调。
