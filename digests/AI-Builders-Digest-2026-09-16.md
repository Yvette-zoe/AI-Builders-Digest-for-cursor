### AI & I by Every: How a Professional Writer Writes With AI

**The Takeaway:** Every staff writer Katie Parrott argues good AI writing is not magic autocomplete; it is a compound system of durable context, human "last mile" facts after the knowledge cutoff, and reusable review skills, so you give feedback once and the model keeps getting better.

**要点：** Every 员工作者 Katie Parrott 认为好的 AI 写作不是魔法补全，而是一套可复利系统：持久 context、人在 knowledge cutoff 之后补上的 “last mile” 事实，以及可复用的审稿 skills，让你只给一次反馈，模型下次就更懂。

Hosted by Natalia Quintero (with an intro from Dan Shipper), the episode previews Every's experimental Compound Writing plugin, inspired by Kieran Klaassen's Compound Engineering and built by Parrott for internal writing workflows. Parrott's path started as an AI skeptic who used ChatGPT as a $20/month career coach after a layoff because a human coach was unaffordable; prompting became a form of strategic writing that clarified her thinking without handing her ready-made answers. As a freelancer she learned early "context engineering": feed brand messaging, audience, product specifics, and competitive differentiators first, then iterate, creating "a fenced in place for the model to play."

由 Natalia Quintero 主持（Dan Shipper 开场），这期预告 Every 实验性插件 Compound Writing：灵感来自 Kieran Klaassen 的 Compound Engineering，由 Parrott 为内部写作流程打造。她最初是 AI 怀疑论者，失业后用每月 $20 的 ChatGPT 当 career coach（请不起真人教练）；prompting 变成一种策略性写作，帮她理清思路，但答案仍要自己挖。做自由职业时她很早学会 “context engineering”：先喂品牌信息、受众、产品细节与差异化，再迭代，给模型一块 “有围栏的游乐场”。

Her sharpest framing is a last-mile problem in AI writing: models work from stale cutoff data, so humans must supply real-world evidence the model cannot see. Compound Writing adapts engineering-style stages (brainstorm, outline, draft, review) into writing stages, including substantive edit, line edit, and publication-ready pass. She compares the plugin to a gym that makes writers stronger, and ships reviewer skills drawn from frameworks she admires (for example Vonnegut's story rules and Hitchcock's suspense principle). The compounding rule she takes from Compound Engineering: "you should only have to give a piece of feedback once, and if you codify it the right way, it will be there forever." Her thesis for the field: education and access will matter more than ever if AI's multiplicative gains are to reach people outside the usual coastal hubs.

她最锋利的判断是 AI 写作的 last-mile 问题：模型总在过期 cutoff 上工作，人必须补上模型看不到的真实世界证据。Compound Writing 把工程式阶段（brainstorm、outline、draft、review）改写成写作阶段，并拆出大改、逐句润色与上线前终检。她把插件比作让写作者变强的健身房，还把喜欢的框架做成 reviewer skills（例如 Vonnegut 的故事法则、Hitchcock 的悬念原则）。她从 Compound Engineering 带走的复利规则是：你只该给一次反馈，编码对了，它就会永远留下。她对行业的 thesis：若要让 AI 的倍增收益走出沿海科技圈，教育与 access 会比以往任何时候都更重要。
