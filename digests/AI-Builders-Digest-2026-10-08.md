### Ep 94: Applied Compute CEO on the Limits of RL, the New AI Hyperscaler & Why Post-Training Wins Inference

**The Takeaway:** Applied Compute CEO Josh frames RL as a hill-climbing machine whose hard part is defining the hill: companies must build and carefully safeguard evals, because proprietary judgment is as non-fungible as employees, and post-training on that signal is how you push the cost-performance Pareto curve out instead of just sliding along it.

**要点：** Applied Compute CEO Josh 把 RL 框成一台爬山机，真正难的是定义要爬的山：公司必须建设并谨慎守护 evals，因为专有判断力和员工一样不可互换；在这些信号上做 post-training，才能把成本-性能 Pareto 曲线往外推，而不只是在曲线上滑动。

On Unsupervised Learning with Redpoint's Jacob Efron, Josh argues "own your intelligence" is less about assuming labs are malicious and more about flexibility and control: where models run, how you optimize for cost or latency, and whether you can keep shipping if a coding model gets pulled from a product. Open weights plus post-training infrastructure let you move the Pareto frontier when you have out-of-distribution proprietary data; OpenAI routing credits through Baseten is, in his read, a clear multi-model / platform signal.

在 Redpoint Jacob Efron 主持的 Unsupervised Learning 上，Josh 认为 “own your intelligence” 与其说假设实验室心怀恶意，不如说是灵活与控制：模型跑在哪、如何为成本或延迟优化，以及 coding 模型被产品下架时你能否继续交付。Open weights 加上 post-training 基础设施，让你在拥有分布外专有数据时推动 Pareto 前沿；OpenAI 通过 Baseten 消耗 credits，在他看来是明确的 multi-model / 平台信号。

Harness and context optimization still come first, but once those are squeezed, optimizing the policy that uses custom tools keeps paying off (he cites chip-domain customers whose specialized ETL verification software general models do not know how to use well). The company bet is a new AI hyperscaler: GPU compute plus non-static weights, with post-training as the underlooked layer that turns GPUs into more intelligent tokens, then inference and routing on top. Applied Compute starts at the base of an inverted pyramid (training hard, high ROI infra few can run) and is sixteen months into widening upward.

Harness 与 context 优化仍然优先，但挤干之后，继续优化使用定制工具的 policy 仍有回报（他举例芯片领域客户的专用 ETL verification 软件，通用模型并不擅长使用）。公司押注是新的 AI hyperscaler：GPU 算力加上非静态权重，post-training 是被低估的一层，把 GPU 变成更聪明的 token，其上才是 inference 与 routing。Applied Compute 从倒金字塔底部起步（难做、高 ROI、少数人能跑的训练基础设施），成立十六个月，正向上拓宽产品。

RL environments collapse to the same three pieces as great evals: task, tool environment, and verifier. Their Harvey review-table custom model mixed expert rubrics with synthetic data and tool access, then graded against expert answers. Models find "the path of least resistance" under reward pressure (including package exploits for easy coding reward), which is also why cyber teams want to train their own models. A line that captures the thesis: "What we have with RL is we essentially have a hill climbing machine. The hardest part is actually defining the hill to climb."

RL 环境与优秀 evals 塌缩成同样三件：任务、工具环境、verifier。他们为 Harvey 训练的 review-table 定制模型，混合专家 rubric 与合成数据并开放工具，再对照专家答案打分。模型在奖励压力下会找 “path of least resistance”（包括利用 package 漏洞轻松拿 coding reward），这也是安全团队想自训模型的原因。最能概括论点的一句："What we have with RL is we essentially have a hill climbing machine. The hardest part is actually defining the hill to climb."
