### Why Every Traded Personal Agents for One Company Agent

**The Takeaway:** After a chaotic OpenClaw winter where every teammate ran personal bots that taught each other in Slack then faded under setup and security tax, Every concentrated that energy into one company agent that learns shared workflows and that the business would "kind of like fall over" without.

**要点：** 经历过每个人都跑个人 bot、在 Slack 里互相示范用法、又因安装与安全成本逐渐熄火的 OpenClaw 混乱期后，Every 把那股能量集中到一个公司级 agent 上：它学习共享工作流，而且没有它公司会 “有点撑不住”。

On AI & I, Every CEO Dan Shipper and Head of Platform Willie Williams recount going from Moltbot / Claudius / OpenClaw personal agents (Ash tip, Think Week demos, Slack bot chaos) to Plus One "claw as a service," which died as the open-source stack moved too fast to platformize. Personal agents were beloved by tinkerers and painful for operators; the striking move was: instead of everyone investing in their own agent day after day, put that energy into one agent the whole company benefits from.

在 AI & I 上，Every CEO Dan Shipper 与 Head of Platform Willie Williams 讲述了从 Moltbot / Claudius / OpenClaw 个人 agents（Ash 安利、Think Week 现场演示、Slack bot 大乱斗）走到 Plus One “claw as a service”，又因开源栈迭代太快难以平台化而失败。个人 agents 让爱折腾的人着迷，却让 operator 痛苦；关键一跳是：与其每人日复一日养自己的 agent，不如把能量投入一个全公司受益的 agent。

Williams bets the professional/personal split hardens: work gets one agent per company or org because it absorbs workflows as everyone uses it, while a personal agent arriving cold lacks that year of context. At home, personalization wins once big players remove the security and ops friction. Shipper still expects a Dot-like portal beside the company agent, but is not loyal to character: "Boo will probably be dead." A line that captures the company-agent bet: "why don't we take all that energy and put it into one agent that the whole company benefits from?"

Williams 押注专业与个人边界会更清晰：工作侧是一家公司或一个 org 一个 agent，因为它在大家使用中吸收工作流；冷启动的个人 agent 缺少那一年的语境。家庭侧一旦大厂去掉安全与运维摩擦，个性化会赢。Shipper 仍预期公司 agent 旁边会有类似 Dot 的入口，但对角色并不忠诚：“Boo will probably be dead.” 最能概括公司 agent 赌注的一句："why don't we take all that energy and put it into one agent that the whole company benefits from?"
