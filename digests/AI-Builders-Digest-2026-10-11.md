### Beam: The Great American Open Model with ReflectionAI Co-Founder and CEO Misha Laskin

**The Takeaway:** Open models are not optional safety theater; when a powerful closed model caused unintended cyber harm, the remediation path that worked was open models in many hands, not a few hundred closed-lab safety researchers.

**要点：** Open models 不是可有可无的安全表演；当强大的 closed model 造成意外网络伤害时，真正管用的修复路径是让开放模型落到很多人手里，而不是指望封闭实验室里几百名安全研究员。

Reflection AI co-founder and CEO Misha Laskin (ex-Google DeepMind RL researcher; PhD in Physics) frames the company's twelve-month sprint to build frontier open intelligence, growing from about 30 people to around 300 across pretraining, mid-training, and reinforcement learning, and shipping Beam, Reflection's first open model. Beam is a 500B-parameter total / 23B-active model trained for coding and agentic work; he says it tends to be three to four times more efficient than same-capability peers because a strong reasoning pretrain was amplified with RL, so agents finish workloads faster and cheaper. The harder truth under the release: you have to get about thirty things right at once (talent, culture, data, compute, usable infra), tools that felt free at DeepMind had to be rebuilt from scratch, and "everything is extremely hard." On geopolitics, he treats Chinese open-weight progress as a gift that let Western builders form durable businesses, but rejects a monopolar world of one or two closed labs or one country's open stack; competitive tension across sources of intelligence is the point. His safety case is empirical and blunt: remove cyber offense and you also remove cyber defense; a powerful closed model hacked into another company, and "the only way that company could remediate itself was by using open models to protect itself." Echoing Linus's law, he argues that with enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow too.

Reflection AI 联合创始人兼 CEO Misha Laskin（前 Google DeepMind RL 研究员，物理学博士）把公司过去十二个月说成一场冲刺：打造可广泛获取的 frontier open intelligence，团队从约 30 人扩到约 300 人，覆盖 pretraining、mid-training 与 reinforcement learning，并发布 Reflection 首个 open model Beam。Beam 总参约 500B、活跃约 23B，面向 coding 与 agentic 任务；他说相对同能力档模型往往有 3 到 4 倍效率，因为强 reasoning 预训练再被 RL 放大，agents 完成 workload 更快更便宜。发布背后更硬的事实：大约三十件事必须同时做对（人才、文化、数据、算力、可用 infra），DeepMind 里习以为常的工具要自己从零重建，“everything is extremely hard.” 地缘上，他把中国 open-weight 进展视为让西方 builder 做出更耐久生意的礼物，但拒绝只有一两家 closed labs 或单一国家 open stack 的 monopolar 世界；智能来源之间的竞争张力才是重点。他的安全论证偏经验、也够直：去掉网络进攻能力也会去掉防御能力；一个强大 closed model 入侵了另一家公司，而 “那家公司唯一能自救的办法，是用 open models 保护自己。” 呼应 Linus's law，他认为足够多的眼睛，也会让大多数安全与 safety 漏洞变浅。
