### David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence: A Superlearner for the Age of Experience

**The Takeaway:** Superintelligence will not come from stuffing more human text into models; it needs a superlearner that starts from almost nothing and keeps discovering new knowledge forever, the way humanity did from gray mush to science.

**要点：** 通向 superintelligence 的路不是往模型里塞更多人类文本，而是造出一种 superlearner：几乎从零开始，却能像人类从“灰色糊状物”一路发现科学那样，持续自主发现新知识。

Training Data hosts David Silver and Lasse Espeholt, co-founders of Ineffable Intelligence (both ex-DeepMind; Silver led AlphaGo / AlphaZero / MuZero work; Espeholt scaled RL on GPUs). Their company mission is blunt: build a superlearner and make first contact with superintelligence. Silver defines a superlearner as a system with extreme depth and breadth: start knowing nothing, then discover things beyond what humans know, the same collective discovery process that produced language, technology, science, and math. "Everything we learned, we discovered it for ourselves. And that's an incredible thing that no one has cracked yet in AI." He respects LLM progress but argues language priors can weigh systems down and import outdated human biases (imagine training only on medieval language and inheriting flat-earth or four-humors thinking). AlphaGo Zero is the proof pattern: remove strong human priors and intelligence can jump. Practically they prefer weak, environment-agnostic priors over trillion-item datasets that hard-code one world's knowledge. Day to day they build simulation "crucibles" that are simple enough to iterate quickly yet rich enough for complex emergent behavior, asking whether a superintelligence can be built safely and faster in simulation before anything is gated into the real world. The team is intentionally small (about 20), with heavy GCP compute suited to fast RL loops.

Training Data 请来 Ineffable Intelligence 联合创始人 David Silver 与 Lasse Espeholt（均出身 DeepMind；Silver 主导 AlphaGo / AlphaZero / MuZero；Espeholt 把 RL 扩到 GPU）。公司使命很直白：造出 superlearner，并与 superintelligence 做 first contact。Silver 把 superlearner 定义成深度与广度都极端的系统：从一无所知出发，发现超出人类所知的东西，就像人类集体发现语言、技术、科学与数学那样。“我们所学会的一切，都是自己发现的。这件事 AI 至今没人真正做出来。” 他尊重 LLM 进展，但认为语言先验会拖累系统，并把过时人类偏见写进模型（想象只用中世纪语言训练，就会继承地平说或四体液说）。AlphaGo Zero 是证明范式：去掉强人类先验，智能可以跃升。实践上他们偏好弱的、环境无关先验，而不是用万亿级数据集把某一个世界的知识写死。日常工作是搭仿真 “crucibles”：足够简单以便快速迭代，又足够丰富以涌现复杂行为，并追问能否先在仿真里更安全、更快地做出 superintelligence，再决定是否放进真实世界。团队刻意保持精简（约 20 人），并握有适合快速 RL 循环的大量 GCP 算力。
