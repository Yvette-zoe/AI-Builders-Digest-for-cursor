### The Changing Physics of Cybersecurity and Drug Development with OpenAI President Greg Brockman

**The Takeaway:** As software gets cheap, value shifts to software you can believe in; cybersecurity spend should vastly outstrip software spend, while drug discovery is the clearest quality-of-life bet if data and wet-lab bottlenecks can be cracked.

**要点：** 软件变便宜之后，价值转向「你信得过」的软件；网络安全支出应大幅超过软件支出，而若能打通数据与湿实验瓶颈，drug discovery 是最清晰的生活质量赌注。

No Priors hosts Regina and Elad talk with OpenAI cofounder and president Greg Brockman after a week that bundled GPT-6 scale, math milestones, Astra, and DOTS. Brockman's operating line: "it never gets easier, you just go faster." On math, he treats recent results as proof AI can generate genuinely new knowledge (hundreds of important open problems resolved), while insisting the point is not opaque proofs but human-legible insights, with debate and iterative amplification as paths to keep people in the oversight loop. Transferring that capability to biology hinges on data: math and theoretical physics have dense data; biology is bottlenecked. Some steps fall fast (he cites regulatory filings that can take a year and become far easier with AI); elsewhere wet labs and simulation matter. Drug discovery is his clearest "revolutionize this" pick: neural nets have no first-principles reason to be better at language than at biology's language, and short-run wins include n-of-1 diseases where the relevant paper already exists. He also describes DOTS as a proactive always-on workplace agent (form factor change more than raw capability), generative UI on the fly, and a cyber "defender's window": peak frontier models help defenders before offense fully diffuses, via programs like Daybreak and a billion-dollar defender commitment. Internally OpenAI paused projects for ~25 production engineers to saturate vulnerabilities with peak models, then expects to repeat each release. Quote that carries the cyber thesis: "cyber spend, security spend should actually vastly outstrip software spend." On mission: humans stay firmly in the driver's seat; technology should amplify human agency.

No Priors 主持人 Regina 与 Elad 采访 OpenAI 联合创始人兼总裁 Greg Brockman：一周里叠了 GPT-6 规模、数学里程碑、Astra 与 DOTS。Brockman 的运转口令是：「it never gets easier, you just go faster。」在数学上，他把近期结果当作 AI 能产出真正新知识的证据（约数百个重要开放问题被解决），同时强调重点不是难懂的证明，而是人能读懂的洞察，并用 debate 与 iterative amplification 把人留在 oversight 环内。把同样能力迁到生物学，关键在数据：数学与理论物理数据稠密，生物学是瓶颈。有些步骤会很快塌掉（他举例写临床试验监管材料可能耗时一年，用 AI 会容易得多）；别处则要靠湿实验与仿真。Drug discovery 是他最明确想「彻底改写」的方向：神经网络没有第一性原理理由证明自己该更擅长自然语言而非生物学语言；短期还可放大 n-of-1 疾病场景（相关论文其实已在文献里）。他还把 DOTS 描述成主动、常开的职场 agent（更多是形态变化而非纯能力跃迁），支持即时 generative UI，并谈网络安全的 「defender's window」：在进攻能力全面扩散前，用 frontier 峰值模型帮防守方，经由 Daybreak 等项目与十亿美元级防守承诺。OpenAI 内部曾让约 25 名生产工程师暂停项目，用峰值模型把漏洞扫到饱和，并预期每次发版重复这一流程。支撑网络论点的原话：「cyber spend, security spend should actually vastly outstrip software spend。」使命上：人类必须牢牢坐在驾驶座；技术应放大 human agency。
