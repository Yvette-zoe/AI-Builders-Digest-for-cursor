**Box CEO Aaron Levie** flips the usual bitter-lesson worry for applied AI: as models get better at long-running tasks and tool use, domain differentiation should rise, not fall. Early limited models forced thin wrappers and light RAG; agent workflows that run for hours or days, coordinate swarms, route by domain, and require real change management make specialized systems more valuable in security, life sciences, healthcare, finance, manufacturing, and beyond.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 把 applied AI 层的 bitter lesson 问题反过来看：模型越能做长任务、越好用工具，按领域做差异化的空间不降反升。早期能力弱时你只能做薄 wrapper 或轻量 RAG；一旦有能跑数小时甚至数天的 agent、需要协调的 agent swarms、按领域路由、还要真正改工作流而不是「往聊天框打字」，安全、生命科学、医疗、金融、制造等领域的专业化系统会更有价值。
