### Taking on ServiceNow: Coding Agents the Rest of Us with Serval Co-Founder and CEO Jake Stauch

**The Takeaway:** Enterprise agents will not scale on raw autonomy; the product is the boundary, code-gen under the hood with approvals, audit trails, and least-privilege escalation that gets more essential as permissions tighten.

**The Takeaway：** 企业级 agent 不可能靠裸奔自治规模化；产品本身就是边界：底层 code-gen，加上审批、审计轨迹与 least-privilege 提权；权限越收紧，这层就越关键。

No Priors hosts Sarah Guo and Elad Gil talk with Serval co-founder and CEO Jake Stauch, who set out from a living-room day-one plan to beat ServiceNow rather than bolt an AI layer onto the incumbent. The bet: workflow automation should be expressed as code, but drag-and-drop builders are too complex for nontechnical users and too abstracted for technical ones; once AI can write the code, a better automation platform becomes possible for IT teams that most companies actually have instead of software engineering departments. Serval treats IT like adults, exposes code under the hood without forcing everyone to read it, and ships Catalyst, an admin agent that turns requirements into platform primitives, policies, and approvals.

No Priors 主持人 Sarah Guo 与 Elad Gil 对话 Serval co-founder and CEO Jake Stauch。他从客厅两人创业的第一天就定下击败 ServiceNow 的目标，而不是在 incumbent 上贴一层 AI。核心判断：企业自动化应以代码表达，但拖拽式 workflow builder 对非技术用户太复杂、对技术用户又抽象过头；一旦 AI 能写代码，就能给多数公司真正拥有的 IT 团队（而不是软件工程部）做出更好的自动化平台。Serval 把 IT 当成年人对待，底层代码可见但不必人人去读，并推出管理端 agent Catalyst，把需求翻译成平台原语、策略与审批。

Stauch frames unbounded agent autonomy as the ultimate enterprise moonshot and also an unbounded security risk: competent agents that can write code and act across systems are not something large firms will babysit-free anytime soon. "Serval is the boundary. Serval is the guardrails." He expects enterprises to shrink end-user access toward true least privilege because agents amplify the blast radius of slightly elevated permissions; Serval then becomes the exception-granting layer with tickets and audit trails, growing more essential as clamps tighten. Pricing leans toward workflows run rather than tokens, because tokens track verbosity more than work done, and CIOs are already asking for proof of ROI after the early token-maxing wave.

Stauch 把「无人看管、自动发现问题并解决」的 background agent 称为企业软件终极 moonshot，同时也是无界安全风险：能写代码并跨系统行动的能干 agent，大企业短期内不会放心放养。"Serval is the boundary. Serval is the guardrails." 他预期企业会把终端用户权限收到真正的 least privilege，因为 agent 会放大「略高一点」权限的破坏半径；Serval 成为带工单与审计的例外授权层，收得越紧就越不可替代。定价倾向按 workflows run 而非 tokens，因为 token 更像啰嗦程度而不是完成的工作；CIO 们在早期 token-maxing 之后，已经在追问 ROI 证据。

The company is about two and a half years old and grew from roughly 20 people in January to about 200, with Stauch arguing more productive employees create demand for more hires, not fewer, because ambition and competition are unbounded. Consumer personal agents preview the enterprise pattern: everyone gets an agent that talks to other agents at work, likely separate from a personal agent the way corporate email differs from Gmail, and the tension between employee desire and enterprise governance is the opportunity. Memorable line: "Serval is the boundary. Serval is the guardrails."

公司约两岁半，从一月约 20 人扩到约 200 人；Stauch 认为员工生产力越高，反而更要招人，因为野心与竞争没有上限。消费级个人 agent 是企业形态的预告：每个人都会有一个在工作中与其他 agent 对话的 agent，很可能像公司邮箱之于 Gmail 那样与个人 agent 分离，而员工欲望与企业治理之间的张力正是机会。难忘原话："Serval is the boundary. Serval is the guardrails."
