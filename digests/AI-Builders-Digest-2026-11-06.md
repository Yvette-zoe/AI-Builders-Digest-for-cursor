### The Inside Story of the 50 Who Built Modern AI With Kevin Roose (AI & I by Every)

**The Takeaway:** After sitting with about 50 builders (and roughly 150 people around them), Kevin Roose does not think we are doomed - but the next few years hinge on choices we make now, starting with boring table-stakes safety testing before models ship to the public.

**The Takeaway：** 在接触大约 50 位建造者（以及周围约 150 人）之后，Kevin Roose 并不认为我们注定完蛋 - 但未来几年取决于我们现在的选择，第一步是模型面向公众之前就要有无聊却必要的安全测试。

On AI & I by Every, former New York Times writer and Machine Gods co-host Kevin Roose talks through *The AGI Chronicles*, his New York Times bestselling account of how modern AI was built. A couple of years ago people had to fact-check the AIs; now "the AIs fact check me," and he has "agents now that are, like, correcting my other agents. It's a huge mess." He wrote the book partly to settle whether we are careening toward an existential cliff or living on hype, and came away believing there are real risks and hard needles to thread - but nothing is predestined: "the choices that we make right now matter a lot."

在 Every 的 AI & I 上，前《纽约时报》作者、Machine Gods 联合主持人 Kevin Roose 讲述畅销书《The AGI Chronicles》里现代 AI 是怎么建成的。几年前人还得 fact-check AI；现在「the AIs fact check me」，他甚至有「agents now that are, like, correcting my other agents. It's a huge mess」。写书部分是为了弄清我们是不是冲向存在主义悬崖，还是只是被 hype 裹挟；他的结论是风险真实、针眼难穿 - 但没有什么是注定的：「the choices that we make right now matter a lot。」

On governance he is blunt that federal AI regulation is unlikely right now, and that governments historically move after crises. His preferred first step is an industry certification bar: before releasing or deploying a model publicly, subject it to standard safety testing against autonomous cyber attacks and novel bioweapons. "That seems like basic table stakes to me. We require pharmaceutical companies to test their drugs before selling them to the public. We should require AI companies to test their models before offering them to the public." He has also newly become convinced that frontier labs may actually want coordination that buys safety teams time - after hearing leaders including Dario, Demis, and Sam say the pace feels too fast.

谈到治理，他直言眼下不太可能有联邦 AI 监管，政府历史上往往在危机后才动。他更想要的第一步是行业认证门槛：公开发布或部署模型前，先做针对自主网络攻击与新型生物武器的标准安全测试。「That seems like basic table stakes to me. We require pharmaceutical companies to test their drugs before selling them to the public. We should require AI companies to test their models before offering them to the public。」他最近也被说服：在听到 Dario、Demis、Sam 等认为节奏太快之后，frontier 实验室或许真的希望有协调机制，给安全团队争取时间。

One artifact he loves is Dario Amade's 2017 OpenAI memo on the "big blob of compute hypothesis," written before large language models even existed: stop training narrow systems for single games, assemble unstructured masses of compute, point them at general problems like next-word prediction, and test whether intelligence falls out of scale. He also traces how *The Making of the Atomic Bomb* shaped Dario's self-image around Leo Szilard - build the technology in part so the wrong actors do not get there first.

他特别喜欢的史料之一，是 Dario Amade 2017 年还在 OpenAI 时写下的「big blob of compute hypothesis」备忘录，当时大语言模型甚至还不存在：别再为单个游戏训窄系统，组装非结构化的算力大团，对准「预测下一个词」这类通用问题，检验智能会不会随规模涌现。他还追踪到《The Making of the Atomic Bomb》如何塑造 Dario 对 Leo Szilard 的自我投射 - 部分是为了先把技术做出来，不让错误的一方抢先。

For personal use he sorts work into weightlifting versus forklifting. Weightlifting is where struggle is the point - bringing a forklift into the gym helps nobody. Forklifting is moving heavy pallets from A to B when a shortcut is the whole job. He weightlifts parenting and forklifts the legal and process grind of starting a company. Tools in rotation include Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, ChatGPT Dot, Town, weekend vibe-coding projects, local models for transcription/summarization, and Granola into a personal CRM - while the actual writing still mostly happens in Google Docs. He and Casey Newton also launched Machine Gods with NPR, treating AI as the most important story in the world.

个人用法上，他把事情分成 weightlifting 与 forklifting。Weightlifting 是挣扎本身就是收益 - 把叉车开进健身房对谁都没好处。Forklifting 则是把沉重托盘从 A 搬到 B，捷径本身就是工作。他在 parenting 上坚持 weightlifting，把创业里的法律与流程苦活交给 forklift。工具轮换包括 Claude、ChatGPT、Gemini、ChatGPT Dot、Town、周末 vibe coding、本地转录/摘要模型，以及 Granola 进个人 CRM - 真正写作大多仍在 Google Docs。他和 Casey Newton 也与 NPR 推出了 Machine Gods，把 AI 当成世界上最重要的新闻。
