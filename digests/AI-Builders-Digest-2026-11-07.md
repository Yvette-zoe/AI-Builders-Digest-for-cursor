### Ep 97: Jev Creator on Decision Models, Benchmark Fatigue & Why the Labs Missed It (Unsupervised Learning)

**The Takeaway:** Treat intelligence like production software, not a demo treadmill: system-one decision models can be hugely useful if you admit their limits, stop worshipping the benchmark of the day, and design for provider-agnostic reliability instead of "maybe the next model will fix it."

**The Takeaway：** 把智能当成生产软件，而不是 demo 跑步机：system one 决策模型可以非常有用，前提是承认局限、别再膜拜当天的 benchmark，并按「提供商无关」的可靠性来设计，而不是指望「下一个模型也许能修好」。

On Unsupervised Learning, the Jev Creator (ASR names the guest Jojo / Jogo, CEO of TypeSave) talks about decision models after JEV's launch storm. Early valuations that price "building god" as infinite look miscalibrated to him; "the let them cure cancer is going to be like Marie Antoinette's, like, let them eat cake." He works backwards from a cognitive core that software can call - LLMs as an API/submodule inside a harness - and is blunt that JEV is "not yet there."

在 Unsupervised Learning 上，Jev Creator（ASR 称嘉宾为 Jojo / Jogo，TypeSave CEO）在 JEV 发布风暴之后谈决策模型。在他看来，把「造神」估成无穷大的早期估值是错位的；「the let them cure cancer is going to be like Marie Antoinette's, like, let them eat cake。」他从软件可调用的认知核心倒推 - LLM 像 harness 里的 API/子模块 - 并直言 JEV「not yet there」。

The contrarian north star is not speed or cheap tokens: "why can we solve Millennium Prize problems in math but not automate anything touching accounting?" He labels these models system one on purpose, even though people treat that as an insult, because honest communication of weakness is what makes them valuable inside software. Chatbots and coding agents still matter - throwaway dashboards are a great coding-agent use case - but overusing them for every task is the failure mode. The intelligence north star should feel like SQL: reliable enough to be provider agnostic, not locked to things that "only work with JEV."

反直觉的北极星不是速度或更便宜的 token：「为什么我们能解数学 Millennium Prize 问题，却自动化不了任何碰会计的事？」他故意把这类模型叫 system one（即便常被当贬义），因为诚实说出弱点，它们在软件里才真正有价值。Chatbot 与 coding agent 仍然重要 - 随手做可丢弃 dashboard 就是 coding agent 的好用例 - 但把它们滥用于一切任务才是失败模式。智能的北极星应像 SQL：可靠到提供商无关，而不是锁死在「只在 JEV 上好用」的东西里。

He is anti-benchmarks for developer welfare: when the market optimizes demos and scores that skip liability, people without time to evaluate models themselves get swept up. He also hates the "update to the latest model" culture the way engineers hate dependency upgrades in production: the treadmill exists because nothing yet runs in the background forever and only needs a revisit every few months. Looking ahead he wants coding agents freer from "the tyranny of the KV cache," and more research-product collaboration that funds missing shapes of software instead of waiting passively for labs. The company name tracks Jevan's paradox: rebound demand from cheapness, yes; full backfire that enlarges the total market the way the paradox predicts, not yet.

出于开发者福祉，他对 benchmark 持反对态度：市场若只优化 demo 与不计责任的分数，没时间亲自评测模型的人就会被卷走。他也讨厌「总要更新到最新模型」的文化，就像工程师讨厌生产环境里升级依赖：这台跑步机之所以存在，是因为还没有东西能在后台永远跑稳、只需要隔几个月再看一眼。展望上，他想要更少受「KV cache 暴政」束缚的 coding agents，以及更多能资助缺失软件形态的研究-产品协作，而不是被动等实验室。公司名对着 Jevan’s paradox：因便宜而来的 rebound 需求，有；悖论所预言的、把总市场做大的完整 backfire，还没有。
