**Latent Space / Controlling Biology - Fabian Theis, Helmholtz Munich**

**The Takeaway:** Biology AI is moving from mapping cells to predicting them to steering the next experiment, and the hard part is not another paper-ending model but a lab culture that will actually run the model's next question.

**要点：** 生物 AI 正在从“绘制细胞”走到“预测细胞”，再走到“引导下一次实验”；真正难的不是再发一篇以模型收尾的论文，而是让实验室文化真的去跑模型提出的下一个问题。

Fabian Theis leads AI and computational biology efforts at Helmholtz Munich and is behind tools most computational biologists already touch (Scanpy, Anndata, scVerse). With hosts RJ Hanocki (CTO of Mira Omics; ASR spelling) and Brandon Anderson (Atomic AI), he frames the virtual-cell holy grail as predicting how a cell behaves under all kinds of perturbations across more than RNA, while noting pharma still cannot run cell-type-context-specific screens at the scale it needs.

Fabian Theis 在 Helmholtz Munich 主持 AI 与计算生物学工作，也是 Scanpy、Anndata、scVerse 等几乎每个计算生物学家都用过的工具背后的人。他与主持人 RJ Hanocki（Mira Omics CTO；ASR 拼写）和 Brandon Anderson（Atomic AI）一起，把 virtual cell 的圣杯定义为：在不只是 RNA 的多层分子规格下，预测细胞在各类扰动中的行为；同时指出制药行业仍无法按细胞类型上下文、以所需规模做筛选。

He is blunt on baselines: in a field rushing for the virtual-cell gold pot, people skipped careful holdouts; even today, close-to-SOTA cell-type prediction inside an organ can be logistic regression (Cell Typist), and his own older paper was literally titled that deep learning is not needed for that job. Cross-organ and harder tasks need more; Arc Institute's "virtual cell challenge" was "a glass of cold water," with fixed metrics that could be exploited, while the Open Problems consortium pushed submitting your own metrics too.

他对 baseline 很直白：领域冲向 virtual cell 金矿时，很多人没认真做 holdout；即便今天，器官内接近 SOTA 的细胞类型预测仍可能只是逻辑回归（如 Cell Typist），他自己更早的论文标题就写着这类任务不需要深度学习。跨器官和更难任务才需要更强模型；Arc Institute 的 “virtual cell challenge” 像一盆冷水，固定指标可被钻空子，而 Open Problems 联盟则推动大家额外提交自己的指标。

The forward move is Lab Compass, an inverse generative model that, given a forward world model, tells you what to do to reach a desired state. Theis ran three or four lab-in-the-loop cycles with a Cambridge hematopoiesis collaborator; convincing experimentalists to spend calendar time on a model's strange next ask is "far from trivial," robotics is "a tad too early" while assay cycles stay long, and the ultimate vision is RL in the loop even if today it is closer to active learning.

向前走的是 Lab Compass：一种 inverse generative model，在已有 forward world model 时告诉你要做什么才能到达目标状态。Theis 与剑桥做造血研究的合作者跑了三到四轮 lab-in-the-loop；说服实验同事把宝贵周期押在模型提出的奇怪下一步上“远非易事”，在 assay 周期仍然偏长时上机器人还“有点太早”，终极愿景是把 RL 放进闭环，尽管眼下更接近 active learning。
