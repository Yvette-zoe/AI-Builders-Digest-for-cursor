**AI & I by Every — A New Vision for Human Work After Automation**

The Takeaway: when everyone has the same model, the scarce asset is not generation but the perspective and responsibility that turn millions of latent answers into one action only you would rank first.

核心结论：当人人都能用同一套模型时，稀缺的不是生成，而是那种能把百万条潜在答案筛成 “只有你会排第一并下手” 的视角与责任。

Every CEO Dan Shipper closed Every's first Thesis conference (ASR also hears "V.C.S.S.") in Brooklyn by contrasting two ways to predict the future of work. The aggregate gathers lots of data and extrapolates a trend line: on OpenAI's GDPval, AI went from beating human experts 12.4% of the time in 2024 to around 83% in 2026, which looks like human work going to zero. The intimate zooms into one messy case and extrapolates from there. With one Claude subscription in a week he analyzed a customer survey, built a custom CRM, made a video game, and personally reached out to a large set of customers: "in pre-AI terms, he did the work of about five people."

Every CEO Dan Shipper 在 Brooklyn 的首届 Thesis 大会（ASR 也听成 “V.C.S.S.”）闭幕演讲里，对比了预测未来工作的两种方法。aggregate 收集大量数据再外推：OpenAI 的 GDPval 上，AI 从 2024 年 12.4% 次打败人类专家，升到 2026 年约 83%，看起来像人类工作归零。intimate 则放大一个真实、messy 的案例再外推。他用一个 Claude 订阅，一周内分析客户问卷、搭自定义 CRM、做游戏，并亲自触达大量客户：“用前 AI 的说法，他干了大约五个人的活。”

Give the same survey and models to the whole Every team and the insights diverge: Yash saw first-time subscribers churning from too much email; Douglas noticed lonely readers excited about AI while people around them think they are crazy; Jack turned results into a Claymation game show. Head of evals Mike Taylor tried to predict what Dan would say in Slack: Claude can include Dan's answer 93% of the time if it may emit 400 candidates, but when asked to rank, Dan's answer lands in the top 10% only around 60% of the time. "Generation is really cheap, but ranking is hard."

把同一份问卷和同一批模型交给整个 Every 团队，洞察会分叉：Yash 看到新订户因邮件太多流失；Douglas 注意到对 AI 兴奋、身边人却觉得他们疯了的孤独读者；Jack 把结果做成黏土动画游戏秀。evals 负责人 Mike Taylor 试预测 Dan 在 Slack 会说什么：Claude 若可输出 400 个候选，93% 能把 Dan 的答案包含进去；但要它排序时，Dan 的答案大约只有 60% 落在 top 10%。“生成真的很便宜，排序很难。”

That is why teams stay valuable: each human owns a slice of the world, which narrows where they prompt, and their perspective lights up which of millions of latent paths is worth taking responsibility for. The common objection (models will rank better next year) is a rigged comparison of 2026 Yash vs 2027 Claude. Cancel the model on both sides and you are choosing 2027 Yash-with-a-model over the default prompt. Once capability is widely distributed it becomes the floor; alpha is pushing past the default with your non-consensus view. Shipper's flip for measurement: stop only asking how good models are at consensus problems everyone agrees are worth solving; ask how good they are at doing your work from your perspective. Or, as he put the allocation-economy thesis: "You won't be judged on how much you know, but instead on how well you can allocate and manage the resources to get work done, the resources of intelligence."

这就是团队仍有价值的原因：每个人对世界的一块负责，从而收缩 prompt 往哪看；视角再点亮百万条潜在路径里哪一条值得担责。常见反驳（明年模型会更会排序）是拿 2026 Yash 对比 2027 Claude 的作弊比较。两边都约掉模型后，你其实是在选 “带着模型的 2027 Yash” 还是默认 prompt。能力一旦广泛分发，就变成地板；alpha 来自用非共识视角把模型推过默认答案。Shipper 对衡量方式的翻转：别只问模型在人人都同意值得解的问题上有多强；要问它在做你的工作时、从你的视角看有多好。或者用他的 allocation economy 表述：“别人不会再按你知道多少来评判你，而会看你多会分配和管理把事情做成的智能资源。”
