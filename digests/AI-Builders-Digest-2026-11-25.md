**Amjad Masad (CEO, Replit)**

Replit CEO Amjad Masad publishes early research on scaling laws for agent swarms: more agents can yield dramatic gains, but more compute does not always help and organization matters. In a fixed two-hour window, Opus 5.5 went from 3.7x NumPy speedup with one agent to 18.9x with 32; GPT-6.2 Astra was strongest solo but showed negative scaling beyond 8; decentralized swarms beat centralized ones, and individual model intelligence poorly predicted collective intelligence. Replit is open-sourcing Optimization-Bench (https://frontier.replit.com/throwing-headcount-at-the-problem/). Separately, after meeting VP JD Vance on American AI leadership, cybersecurity, and software infrastructure, he warns America could win the frontier race and still lose the global open-weight ecosystem as allies look to Chinese open models.

Replit CEO Amjad Masad 发布 agent swarms 扩展律早期研究：增加 agent 数量可带来显著收益，但更多算力不一定更好，组织方式很关键。在固定两小时窗口里，Opus 5.5 对 NumPy 从单 agent 的 3.7x 加速升到 32 agents 的 18.9x；GPT-6.2 Astra 单兵最强，但超过 8 个后出现负扩展；去中心化 swarm 优于中心化协调，个体模型智力也很难预测集体智力。Replit 开源 Optimization-Bench（https://frontier.replit.com/throwing-headcount-at-the-problem/）。另他与副总统 JD Vance 讨论美国 AI 领导力、网络安全与软件基础设施后警告：美国可能赢下 frontier 竞赛，却仍输掉全球 open-weight 生态，因为盟友正转向中国开源模型。
