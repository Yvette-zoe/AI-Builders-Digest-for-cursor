**Unsupervised Learning / Ep 98: China Expert on Jensen’s Return**

**The Takeaway:** Jensen Huang's reinstatement does not rewind to a pre-crisis steady state; the near-term tell is whether the new remote-access and smuggling-hardening rules stick or get walked back, because the national-security framing of advanced chips is now loud and bipartisan.

**一句话：** Jensen Huang 复职并没有把时钟拨回危机前的稳态；近期观察点是新的 remote-access 与反走私加严规则会卡住还是被收回，因为先进芯片的国家安全叙事已经变成大声且两党都在喊。

China Talk's Jordan Schneider joins Redpoint investor Jacob Efron for an emergency Unsupervised Learning episode on the geopolitics around Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's ouster and return. Schneider compares the comeback to "Napoleon back from Elba" and argues the board only felt safe acting after expecting some mix of settlement and pardons tied to reporting on banned chips reaching China, including PLA-linked research channels such as Mega Speed. His core indictment is not that a CEO wants Chinese hyperscaler demand, but willful blindness to smuggling: years of TV denial that racks are too heavy to move, then Super Micro-linked reporting of labels being stripped for easier transit.

China Talk 的 Jordan Schneider 做客 Redpoint 投资人 Jacob Efron 的 Unsupervised Learning 紧急节目，拆 Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang 被撤又回归背后的地缘政治。Schneider 把复出比作「Napoleon 从 Elba 回来」，并认为董事会敢动，是因为预期会出现与「禁运芯片流入中国、包括 PLA 相关科研渠道（如 Mega Speed）」报道挂钩的和解与赦免组合。他真正重锤的不是 CEO 想要中国云厂商订单，而是对走私的装瞎：电视上多年否认机架重到搬不动，随后出现与 Super Micro 相关的报道，称有人用吹风机撕标签以便转运。

The fresh rule package, as Schneider reads it, hardens smuggling, requires Commerce approval for advanced chips fabbed at TSMC, and after ninety days cuts remote-access neo-clouds in places like Malaysia that currently serve Chinese customers. Efron is more bullish that a federated, weaker Nvidia would have confused the supply chain and that the ecosystem is healthier with Jensen back under the current administration; Schneider's counter is revenue durability plus political risk: a poll he cites says only 2% of Americans want AI to progress faster, and cyber chaos will make that worse before bio anecdotes can rebrand the industry. On the "better they build on our stack" argument, Schneider rejects the "Jensen postulate" that AI is normal tech without national-security bite: after cyber-capable models, "this is a technology that you can use to hack the planet," and Huawei's bottleneck is HBM and semiconductor manufacturing equipment supply, not demand. His operator advice: if you have Tencent as a client or build on Chinese models, do not assume the story ends because of a pardon.

按 Schneider 的解读，新规则组合会加难走私、要求在 TSMC 制造先进芯片须获商务部批准，并在九十天后切断马来西亚等地目前服务中国客户的 remote-access neo-cloud。Efron 更乐观：联邦式、变弱的 Nvidia 会搞乱供应链，Jensen 在本届政府下回归对生态更健康；Schneider 反问收入能否持续，并抬出政治逆风：他引用的民调称只有 2% 美国人希望 AI 发展更快，网络混乱会让这更糟，而生物轶事还撑不起改品牌。对「不如让他们建在我们的栈上」一派，他否定「Jensen postulate」那种把 AI 当普通技术、无关国家安全的假设：在具备网络攻击能力的模型出现后，「这是可以用来黑掉星球的技术」；Huawei 的瓶颈是 HBM 与半导体设备供给，不是需求。给从业者的提醒：如果你有 Tencent 客户或在中国模型上构建，别以为一纸赦免故事就结束了。
