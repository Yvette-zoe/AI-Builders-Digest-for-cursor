Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

Levie argues code is unusually agent-friendly because you can quickly test whether something works; most other knowledge work only gets feedback when it hits the real world. That gap creates a new opportunity: better evals for enterprise workflows, so teams know if a model, prompt, or system change helped or hurt. Enterprises that evaluate knowledge work well will gain the most from agents. He also praised a proposal for an AI standards body (distinct from a regulatory agency), warning that if AI progress starts moving at classic government speed, progress stalls and America risks losing the race.

Levie 认为代码特别适合 agent，因为你可以很快验证结果是否可用；多数其他知识工作要等到真正落地才有反馈。这个差异意味着新机会：为企业工作流建立更好的 eval，才能判断模型、prompt 或系统改动是变好还是变差。能把知识工作评估做好的企业，会从 agent 中获益最多。他也认可一份 AI 标准机构提案（不同于监管机构），并警告若 AI 进步开始按政府惯常节奏推进，进步就会停滞，美国也可能输掉竞赛。

Ryo Lu (Design, Cursor)

In a long reflective post, Cursor designer Ryo Lu writes about what happens "when the dream becomes the job": the private craft that once lived in the margins becomes deadlines, roadmap, and reputation. With AI now approximating writing, coding, design, and taste itself, the scary question is not only jobs but identity: if a machine can do the thing you were good at, what part was really you? His answer: AI can raise the floor, but it cannot want on your behalf. The discipline is returning to the original love, the source of the work, so the fire stays alive.

Cursor 设计师 Ryo Lu 写了一篇长文，谈 "when the dream becomes the job"：曾经只属于自己的手艺，一旦成为工作，就会被 deadline、roadmap 和声誉绑定。当 AI 开始逼近写作、编程、设计和品味本身，可怕的不只是岗位，还有身份：如果机器也能做你擅长的事，哪一部分才真正是你？他的回答是：AI 可以抬高下限，但不能替你去想要。真正的修炼，是不断回到最初的热爱，也就是工作的源头，让那团火还在。

Training Data — Anthropic's Katelyn Lesse & Angela Jiang: Building an Ecosystem, not a Walled Garden

The Takeaway: Anthropic Platform is betting that the durable advantage is not a walled garden of owned infrastructure, but a layered ecosystem of primitives, standards, and optional higher-order agent abstractions that any builder can remix.

The Takeaway：Anthropic Platform 押注的长期优势，不是把基础设施做成围墙花园，而是一层可被任意 builder 重组的 primitives、标准，以及可选的更高阶 agent 抽象。

Katelyn Lesse and Angela Jiang run Anthropic Platform: the externally facing APIs and developer platform for Claude, plus the internal product infrastructure Anthropic's own apps build on. Internally, the north star is speed and leverage so teams can ship "AGI pilled" products. Externally, it is giving any builder the tools to work with Claude wherever their business already lives, including deep hyperscaler integrations.

Katelyn Lesse 与 Angela Jiang 负责 Anthropic Platform：既包括面向外部的 Claude API 与开发者平台，也包括 Anthropic 自家产品所依赖的内部基础设施。对内，北极星是速度与杠杆，让团队能快速交付 "AGI pilled" 产品；对外，则是把工具送到 builder 业务所在之处，并与云厂商深度集成。

A core thesis is that model form factors keep changing too fast to freeze the product shape. Two years ago everything was chat; now it is agents; next year it will be something else. So the platform strategy is democratizing the primitives so the market can discover the next form factor. That shows up as standards like skills and MCP shared with the ecosystem, plus higher-order offerings such as managed agents, sandboxes, session storage, and harness engineering for teams that do not want to rebuild the same agent infrastructure themselves. AI-native startups often stay on raw primitives; enterprises reach for the packaged layers.

核心判断是：模型的产品形态变化太快，不能锁死形态。两年前一切是 chat，现在是 agents，明年又会不同。因此平台策略是开放 primitives，让市场自己发现下一种形态。这体现在向生态开放 skills、MCP 等标准，以及为不想自建 agent 基础设施的团队提供 managed agents、sandbox、session storage、harness engineering 等更高层能力。AI native startup 往往留在原始 primitives；企业则更常选择封装层。

On open ecosystem versus walled garden, they are deliberately not precious about owning every layer of execution. Claude Managed Agents can plug into self-hosted sandboxes and partners like Modal, Vercel, Cloudflare, and Amazon microVMs, and MCP tunnels let agents reach servers behind a firewall. Looking ahead, they describe a ladder from knowledge to execution to coordination: a "meta harness" of strategies where tokens are not fungible, some advise while others execute. As Angela put it on standards and access: transformative tech only becomes utility when "you can actually like wire it into everything," and "that's not something that anybody can do by themselves."

关于开放生态 vs 围墙花园，他们并不执着于拥有执行层的每一块。Claude Managed Agents 可以接入自托管 sandbox，以及 Modal、Vercel、Cloudflare、Amazon microVM 等伙伴；MCP tunnels 也能打通防火墙后的服务。展望未来，他们描述了从 knowledge 到 execution 再到 coordination 的抽象阶梯：一种 "meta harness"，token 不再完全可互换，有的负责建议，有的负责执行。用 Angela 的话说，变革性技术只有在 "you can actually like wire it into everything" 时才真正变成公用事业，而 "that's not something that anybody can do by themselves"。
