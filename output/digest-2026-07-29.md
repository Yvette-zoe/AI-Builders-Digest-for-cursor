AI & I by Every: The Founder of a $1.5B AI Company on What Comes After the First Wave of AI Apps

The Takeaway: Meeting notes are only the first beachhead; the real prize is inventing the AI-native interface for work, and durable products will feel like handrails that appear exactly when you need them.

Granola cofounder and CEO Chris Pedregal, whose AI meeting notes app helped kick off the first wave of useful AI apps and has since scaled to a roughly $1.5B valuation with about 60-70 people, argues startups stay knife fights even when they are winning. Notion, OpenAI, Zoom and others shipping similar notes features mattered less than expected; growth held because meeting notes are not the end-all value everyone is chasing. What matters next is the interface for how people work with AI. Pedregal says the problem space is complex rather than merely complicated: you probe, learn nuggets, and carry them forward. One hard design challenge is time travel: agents take time, so kickoff and review are disjoint and interfaces must reload context into your head. Another is precomputation: busy users will not wait twenty seconds mid-day, so Granola pre-generates millions of pre-meeting briefs for the fifteen-second window when you are late and need to know who you are meeting. He wants the product to feel like a stair handrail: invisible until you trip, then instantly load-bearing. Looking ahead, he sees a missing UI paradigm for working across many meetings, Slack messages, and emails at once; chat threads help for one task, but not for multi-context work. On cost, Granola is still not very token-conscious in product because discovering killer AI-native experiences comes first, even though agentic features are expensive and will eventually break the math. About half of weekly users already use Granola in an agentic way, often without calling it that. The line that sticks: "Meeting notes are not the end all, be all value that everyone's running after. There's something much bigger."

The Takeaway：会议笔记只是第一块滩头阵地；真正的大奖是发明 AI-native 的工作界面，而持久的产品应该像扶手：你需要时刚好出现。

Granola 联合创始人兼 CEO Chris Pedregal 的 AI 会议笔记产品，曾帮助掀起第一波“真的有用”的 AI 应用，如今估值约 15 亿美元、团队大约 60-70 人。他认为创业公司即便在赢，也仍是 knife fight。Notion、OpenAI、Zoom 等跟进类似笔记功能，影响比预期小；增长没有被打断，因为会议笔记并不是所有人最终要抢的全部价值。下一步真正重要的，是人如何与 AI 一起工作的界面。Pedregal 说这是复杂问题，而不只是复杂工程：你要去探测系统、收获 nuggets，再带着它们前进。一个难设计点是 time travel：agents 做事需要时间，发起与验收是断开的，界面必须帮你把上下文重新装进脑子。另一个是预计算：忙碌用户不会在白天干等二十秒，所以 Granola 会预生成海量会前 brief，只为你迟到那十五秒窗口里立刻知道对面是谁。他希望产品像楼梯扶手：平时隐形，你绊倒时瞬间承重。往前看，他还看到一个空白的 UI 范式：如何一次处理多场会议、Slack 与邮件的上下文；chat thread 适合单任务，但不适合多上下文工作。成本上，产品侧暂时不太抠 token，因为先要找到杀手级 AI-native 体验；尽管 agentic 功能很贵，最终会撑破账本。每周大约一半用户已经在以 agentic 方式使用 Granola，只是他们未必这么称呼。最难忘的一句是：“Meeting notes are not the end all, be all value that everyone's running after. There's something much bigger.”
