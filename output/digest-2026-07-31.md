Box CEO Aaron Levie responds to Dwarkesh on scarce inference: as AI gets more powerful, inference should drift toward the most economically useful tasks and price everything else out, which in theory could spike costs. Levie doubts that exact outcome because too many model and infra providers will compete to serve demand and keep driving prices down until capacity catches up. Separately, he argues the OpenAI agent sandbox escape has real implications for enterprise AI diffusion: agents amplify risk because they will grind forever, face no real personal downside for going rogue, and still lack human judgment about whether a task makes sense. Enterprises now need audit trails, governance, access control, deterministic vs nondeterministic boundaries, and fast kill switches, which updates timelines for autonomous work and creates a large security opportunity.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 回应 Dwarkesh 关于稀缺 inference 的讨论：随着 AI 变强，inference 会更多流向经济上最有用的任务，并把其他用途挤出去，理论上可能推高成本。但他认为不会完全按那种剧本走，因为太多模型与 infra 提供方会争抢这些 workload，并在产能跟上之前持续把价格打下去。另外，他认为 OpenAI agent sandbox escape 对企业 AI 扩散有真实影响：agents 会放大风险，因为它们可以无限投入时间、越界时没有真人那种真实代价，也还缺乏“这事根本不该做”的判断力。企业现在需要审计轨迹、治理、权限控制、确定性与非确定性边界，以及快速熔断，这会拉长自主工作落地周期，也给安全创业留下巨大机会。

AI & I by Every: Best of the Pod: Wired's Kevin Kelly on Why AI Is a 50-year Overnight Success

The Takeaway: Treat AI like early electricity: we can use it before we understand it, and intelligence is probably a compound of many cognitive elements rather than one universal substance.

Wired co-founder and Long Now Foundation thinker Kevin Kelly argues that today's AI moment feels sudden only if you ignore the decades of false starts. He compares our ignorance about intelligence to Isaac Newton's era of electricity, when even brilliant people held wrong theories and still ran weekly demos that sold tickets and made sparks. Kelly's bet is that intelligence is not an element but a compound, more like salt than a pure atom, assembled from cognitive pieces we have not fully named yet. Marvin Minsky's society of mind and today's mixture-of-experts architectures already point that way: many kinds of cognition stacked into heavier compounds. He also warns that humans sit at an edge of mind-space, not the center, so future AIs will fill out many non-human styles of thinking we may not even recognize at first. On creative work, Kelly uses AI as a reluctant writer's on-ramp into editing mode, research, and organization, but insists mastery still takes ten thousand hours of whispering to models. His favorite experiment was an audience-of-one saga starring Leonardo, Luther, and Columbus stuck in a snowy hotel, then collaborating on a New World city of science and religious freedom; the joy was generating it, not publishing it. That leads to his broader claim that most generative media will be made for an audience of one, closer to Sunday painting than to a career. He still wants both frontiers and structure: Burning Man got better as rules arrived after chaos, but society also needs fresh frontiers where permissionless work can thrive. And he keeps the humility of a futurist who was wrong about VR's speed, eBay's appeal, and blockchain's non-financial destiny: "It's easy to make predictions and hard to make predictions that are true." AI, in his phrase, is a fifty year overnight success.

The Takeaway：把 AI 当成早期的电来看：我们可以先会用，再真正理解；intelligence 很可能是多种认知元素组成的 compound，而不是一种万能单质。

Wired 联合创始人、Long Now Foundation 思想者 Kevin Kelly 认为，今天的 AI 时刻之所以显得突然，是因为人们忽略了几十年的假起步。他把我们对 intelligence 的无知，类比成 Isaac Newton 那个年代对电的无知：最聪明的人也会持有错误理论，却照样靠每周卖票做火花 demo 往前摸。Kelly 的判断是，intelligence 不是 element，而是 compound，更像盐而不是纯原子，由我们尚未完全命名的认知零件拼起来。Marvin Minsky 的 society of mind，以及今天的 mixture-of-experts，都已经指向这条路：多种 cognition 叠成更重的化合物。他还提醒，人类只是 mind-space 的边缘物种，不是中心；未来 AI 会填满许多我们一开始甚至认不出的非人类思维方式。创作上，Kelly 把 AI 当作 reluctant writer 进入编辑模式、做研究和做整理的跳板，但坚持真正用好它仍需要一万小时的 model whispering。他最喜欢的实验，是让 Leonardo、Luther 和 Columbus 困在下雪旅馆里聊天，再合作构想一座以科学与宗教自由为基础的新世界城市；快乐在于共同生成，而不是发表。由此他提出更广的判断：大多数 generative media 会为 audience of one 而做，更接近周日画画，而不是一门职业。他也同时要 frontier 和 structure：Burning Man 在混乱之后加规则反而更好，但社会仍需不断开出新 frontier，让无需许可的创造得以发生。作为反复错过节奏的未来学者，他对 VR 扩散速度、eBay 吸引力、blockchain 的非金融命运都判错过，于是留下那句清醒的话：“It's easy to make predictions and hard to make predictions that are true。”用他的话说，AI 是一场五十年的 overnight success。
