Training Data: Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed: Why AI Models Stop Learning, and How to Start It Again

The Takeaway: Continual learning is not a niche research topic; it is what learning always was, and frozen internet-scale LLMs are a powerful but incomplete slice of intelligence that still leave the hard problem of learning from experience unsolved.

要点： Continual learning 不是一个窄众课题，而是学习本来就该有的样子；冻结在互联网规模语料上的 LLM 很强，但只覆盖了智能的一部分，真正从经验中持续学习的问题仍未解决。

Rich Sutton (reinforcement learning pioneer, The Bitter Lesson author, University of Alberta) and cofounder Khurram Javed discuss Oak Lab's bet against the current LLM-first orthodoxy. Sutton's 26-word bitter lesson: do not get distracted by human knowledge; focus on learning methods that scale with computation, like search and learning. He calls LLMs both a positive and negative bitter-lesson case: they scaled computation brilliantly by absorbing the internet, then hit the ceiling of finite human text. Javed's "big world hypothesis" says the world is massively larger than any agent, so synthetic data curated by humans remains a bottleneck rather than a path to open-ended competence.

Rich Sutton（强化学习先驱、《The Bitter Lesson》作者、University of Alberta）与联合创始人 Khurram Javed 讨论了 Oak Lab 对抗当前 LLM 优先正统路线的押注。Sutton 用 26 个词概括 bitter lesson：不要被人类知识分心，专注会随算力扩展的学习方法，例如 search 与 learning。他认为 LLM 既是正面也是反面教材：靠吞下互联网把算力用到极致，随后又撞上有限人类文本的天花板。Javed 的 big world hypothesis 认为世界远比任何 agent 复杂，因此由人类策划的 synthetic data 仍是瓶颈，而不是通向开放能力的路径。

The missing capability, Sutton argues, is learning a model and then planning with self-discovered abstractions, not just next-millisecond prediction with a known ruleset like AlphaGo or Lean. Their Alberta Plan makes continual deep learning the unlock: naive single-stream weight updates cause catastrophic forgetting, while batch tricks (as in products that pool many users) do not cover most real streams. The proposed cure mixes per-weight step-size meta-learning with continual backprop that keeps planting new randomly initialized units so generate-and-test does not run out of diversity.

Sutton 认为仍缺失的能力是：学会一个模型，再用自我发现的抽象去做规划，而不是只在 AlphaGo 或 Lean 这类已知规则集上做下一毫秒预测。他们的 Alberta Plan 把 continual deep learning 当作解锁点：对单条经验流做朴素权重更新会 catastrophic forgetting，而靠聚合多用户 batch 的技巧覆盖不了大多数真实场景。药方是把每个权重的 step-size meta-learning，与持续播种随机初始化新单元的 continual backprop 结合起来，让 generate-and-test 不至于耗尽多样性。

Ambition check: they want a spectrum of knowledge from sensorimotor detail to airplane-route abstractions that stays self-correcting instead of frozen after post-training. Javed frames a trillion-parameter mind at ~20 watts as a 5-10 year algorithmic-plus-hardware bet, noting labs are stuck in a local minimum where exploring a worse-before-better paradigm is product-incompatible. Sutton is careful not to dismiss LLMs: fluent language use is an amazing breakthrough, maybe a quarter of intelligence, but "we're not done."

野心校准：他们想要从感觉运动细节到航线级抽象的一整谱知识，并且能自我校正，而不是 post-training 后冻结。Javed 把约 20 瓦驱动万亿参数心智看成 5-10 年的算法加硬件赌注，并指出大实验室困在局部最优，容不下「先变差再变好」的范式探索。Sutton 并不否定 LLM：流畅语言是惊人突破，或许占智能的四分之一左右，但「我们还没做完」。
