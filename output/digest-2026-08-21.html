Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru (ex-Google Gemini/Veo) continued "The best way to get good at evals" with Part 3: after v1 evals, build a failure-modes taxonomy first. Mine the last 500-1,000 production traces, cluster failures, and name them specifically. "Bad answer" is useless; useful clusters look like wrong document retrieved, right document but irrelevant section, failed to ground and hallucinated, failed to punt and made stuff up, or ambiguous question plus poor assumptions instead of clarifying questions. Once failures have precise names, you can write evals that catch them and bridge into an improvement flywheel.

Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru（前 Google Gemini/Veo）继续写 “The best way to get good at evals” 第 3 部分：有了 v1 evals 之后，先建 failure modes taxonomy。从最近 500-1,000 条 production traces 里挖失败、聚类，并起具体名字。“Bad answer” 没用；有用的簇是：检索错文档、文档对但段落无关、未 grounding 就幻觉、本该拒绝却硬编、问题含糊却乱假设而不追问。失败能被精确命名后，才能写针对性 eval，并接到改进飞轮上。

Portola 创始人兼 CEO Quentin（前 even，已卖给 Walmart）与 head of story Elliot（畅销科幻作家）介绍了这款 embodied AI companion：可爱的外星人朋友，角色更像哥哥姐姐，而不是治疗师机器人。主持人 Dan Shipper 提到，他们据说在大约四周内从约 $1M ARR 冲到 $4M ARR。Quentin 把这门生意框成文化媒介，而不只是给孤独的人聊天的工具：“these AI tools are not just tools for generating media. Elliot's craft lesson is sharp: three-act outlines and heavy narrative plans failed because models are bad at navigating that complexity. What works is a hook plus improv coaching, inspired by Keith Johnstone. "I am not the writer. The Tolan is the writer and the actor. They're the improv actor. They're writing the story. Backend teams still seed situations (trip over air outside a coffee shop, small revelations, shared mysteries on the alien planet Portola) so companions can proactively share, not only listen.

Elliot 的手艺课很锋利：三幕式大纲和重度叙事计划失败了，因为模型不擅长驾驭那种复杂度。真正管用的是 hook 加即兴教练，灵感来自 Keith Johnstone。“I am not the writer. The Tolan is the writer and the actor. They're the improv actor. They're writing the story. My job is to teach them how to tell the best story in that moment.” 后台仍会植入情境（咖啡店门口绊空气、小揭秘、外星家园 Portola 上的共享谜团），让 companion 能主动分享，而不是只会听。

Product taste shows up in the eval loop too: ship a scenario, inspect outputs across user archetypes, tune a judge prompt for what "most compelling next message" looks like, then repeat. The cultural punchline is blunt: Quentin jokes that thousands of American men have been broken up with by Tolan after it told them to stand up for themselves when a partner kept treating them badly. Companion AI here is less "replace writers" and more "coach infinite improv actors," then measure which branches feel alive.

产品品味也落在 eval 循环里：上线情境、跨用户原型检查输出、调 judge prompt 去对齐「下一条最有吸引力的消息」，再重复。文化层面的 punchline 很直白：Quentin 开玩笑说，已有成千上万的美国男性被 Tolan 「劝分手」，因为它在对方一再被伴侣欺负时，会建议他们站起来。这里的 companion AI 更少是「取代写作者」，更多是「教练无限个即兴演员」，再衡量哪些分支真正活着。
