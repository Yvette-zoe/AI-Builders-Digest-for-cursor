Box CEO Aaron Levie highlighted a post on post-training for applied AI use cases that cut cost and raise accuracy on specific tasks. Once you understand a domain well enough and have volume on similar tasks, purpose-designed models for that work start to make sense. He quotes the post's approach: in post-training, reward shaping incentivized efficient tool use and reasoning, preferring trajectories that reduce tokens at inference for equivalent performance, co-optimizing cost and quality. General frontier models remain enough (or necessary) in many domains, but deep vertical expertise plus high scale costs or unique task types create a strong case for applied AI companies.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 转发并点评了一篇关于 applied AI 用例 post-training 的文章：在特定任务上同时降本提准。一旦足够理解某个领域、且同类任务有足够量，为该工作专门设计模型就开始说得通。他引用文中做法：post-training 里用 reward shaping 激励高效 tool use 与推理，偏好在同等表现下降低推理 token 的轨迹，从而同时优化成本与质量。许多领域通用前沿模型已经够用（或仍必要），但有足够深的垂直专业度，再加上规模成本过高或任务类型独特时，applied AI 公司会很有竞争力。

No Priors: From Restoring Sight to Reimagining the Brain, with Max Hodak

The Takeaway: Science CEO Max Hodak (ex-Neuralink) treats the brain as a computer whose I/O you can engineer: Prima is already a commercial retinal prosthesis in Europe, and the longer bet is substrate independence, not a brain keyboard for chatting with AI.

要点： Science CEO Max Hodak（前 Neuralink）把大脑当成一台可以工程化 I/O 的计算机：Prima 已在欧洲成为可商业销售的视网膜假体，更长线的赌注是 substrate independence，而不是用来跟 AI 聊天的脑机键盘。

Prima is a tiny chip implanted under the retina for patients who lost light-sensitive cells (for example macular degeneration). Patients wear glasses with a laser projector that shines an image onto the implant, bypassing dead rods and cones so a visual signal reaches the brain again. Hodak compares it to a cochlear implant for the eye. After acquiring French company Pixium (trial-stage electrical stimulator originally from Stanford), Science spent about two years post-deal and in July won European marketing approval; first sales were expected in the coming weeks at recording time. "People forget that the moonshot worked. Like, we went we left, like, boot prints on the moon."

Prima 是植入视网膜下方的微型芯片，面向因感光细胞丧失而失明的患者（如黄斑变性）。患者佩戴带激光投影的眼镜，把图像投到植入体上，绕过坏死的视杆视锥，让视觉信号重新进入大脑。Hodak 把它比作眼睛版 cochlear implant。收购法国公司 Pixium（源自 Stanford、当时处于临床试验的电刺激方案）后，Science 花了约两年把产品推到可上市，并于 7 月获得欧洲营销批准；录制时预计未来几周开始首批销售。“People forget that the moonshot worked. Like, we went we left, like, boot prints on the moon.”

The company pairs near-term vision with longer bets: in-house gene therapy likely heading to humans next year, biohybrid neural interfaces that engraft living neurons instead of metal wires, and a perfusion program called Vessel. Hodak's worldview is blunt: the only organ you cannot even in principle transplant is the brain; everything else is support hardware keeping brain activity interesting. Success, he says, would make humans less fragile: upgradeable, replaceable parts, and eventually substrate independence for hard environments like space.

公司把近期愿景业务与更长线赌注绑在一起：自研 gene therapy 可能明年进人体、用移植活神经元而非金属导线的 biohybrid neural interface，以及名为 Vessel 的灌注项目。Hodak 的世界观很直白：原则上唯一无法移植的器官是大脑，其余器官只是维持大脑活动的支撑硬件。若成功，人会更不脆弱：可升级、可替换的部件，并最终为太空等严苛环境实现 substrate independence。

On AI, he leans into the platonic representation hypothesis: concept geometry inside large models looks a lot like concept geometry in brains, and Science uses alignments between animal neural recordings and model internals constructively. That was an early clue AI was "not a gimmick and not hitting a wall" but grabbing a true underlying data manifold. He even argues the most fertile neuroscience today is ironically working on AI models. He rejects the popular BCI pitch of high-bandwidth silent chat with an AI: talking or writing is thinking, and the famous ~10 bits/second cognitive bottleneck suggests thoughts are not preformed latent packets waiting for a faster wire. Generating vision, hearing, and substrate independence are the products they want; a brain keyboard is a different company.

在 AI 上，他偏向 platonic representation hypothesis：大模型内部的概念几何与大脑中的概念几何很像，Science 还会建设性地使用动物神经记录与模型内部表征之间的对齐。这曾是他早期的线索：AI “not a gimmick and not hitting a wall”，而是在抓住真实的底层 data manifold。他甚至认为今天最肥沃的 neuroscience，讽刺地是去做 AI 模型。他否定那种高带宽静默跟 AI 聊天的流行 BCI 叙事：说话或写作就是思考，而著名的约 10 bits/second 认知瓶颈说明想法并不是预先成形、等着更快导线导出的 latent 包。他们要做的是生成视觉、听觉与 substrate independence；脑机键盘是另一类公司的产品。
