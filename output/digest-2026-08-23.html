Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru (ex-Google Gemini/Veo) published "How to build great evals - part 5": stop collapsing a complex eval suite into one score for a senior stakeholder. That "tyranny of the average" hides regressions on frontier work. His example: simple summarization 85%→89%, basic factual QA 80%→85%, complex financial analysis 70%→63%. A single number looks like progress while the hard use case got worse. Weighted scores just wrap a judgment call in fake mathematical precision. Instead keep a prioritized eval ladder, put people on the details, and decide better/worse from the critical failures and wins, not an abstracted average.

Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru（前 Google Gemini/Veo）发布 “How to build great evals - part 5”：别把复杂 eval 套件压成一个分数去伺候高层。这种 “tyranny of the average” 会掩盖前沿任务上的回退。他举例：简单 summarization 85%→89%、基础 factual QA 80%→85%、复杂 financial analysis 70%→63%。单一分数看起来像进步，真正难的用例却变差了。加权分只是给主观判断套上假的数学精确度。正确做法是保留优先级 eval 阶梯，让人看细节，从关键失败与亮点判断更好还是更差，而不是看抽象均值。
