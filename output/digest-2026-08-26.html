Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru (ex-Google Gemini/Veo) published "How to build great evals - part 8" on the discriminatory property of evals. A hill-climbing eval only helps when it separates systems that are meaningfully different: scores like 94/93/95/94/92 are useless if you already know A and C beat D and E, because the eval never said so. Making the test arbitrarily hard so everyone fails is also wrong. The sweet spot is realistic, difficult, and sensitive to capability gaps; as harnesses and models improve, good evals saturate, and the open question is what you do next.

Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru（前 Google Gemini/Veo）发布 “How to build great evals - part 8”，讲 eval 的 discriminatory property。hill-climbing eval 只有在能区分“真正有差异”的系统时才有用：如果分数是 94/93/95/94/92，而你已经知道 A、C 明显强于 D、E，那这个 eval 几乎没告诉你任何事。把测试任意做难、让所有人挂掉也不对。甜点是 realistic + difficult + 对能力差异敏感；随着 harness 与底层模型变强，好的 eval 会饱和，下一步该怎么处理仍是开放问题。

Box CEO Aaron Levie argued systems of record matter more than ever once AI agents do 100X more work on those platforms than people ever did: agents will query data, run workflows, and collaborate with humans and other agents, so governance, reliability, security, access controls, and business logic stay critical (citing the OpenAI Hugging Face incident as an early signal). That only holds for systems that ship agent-ready product experiences, APIs, and business models. Separately he said Zero Data Retention (ZDR) drove a large share of AI growth by simplifying compliance for subprocessors and enterprise PII policies; without ZDR, AI diffusion slows sharply, especially while holdout models keep exception processes alive.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，当 AI agent 在 systems of record 上的工作量达到人类的 100 倍时，这些系统比以往任何时候都更重要：agent 会查数据、跑工作流、与人和其它 agent 协作，因此治理、可靠性、安全、权限与业务逻辑仍然关键（他引用 OpenAI Hugging Face 事件作为早期信号）。这只适用于真正为 agent 提供产品体验、API 与商业模式的系统。另外他指出 Zero Data Retention（ZDR）大幅简化了 subprocessor 合规与企业 PII 政策，从而推动了大量 AI 增长；在仍有模型拒不跟进、例外流程漫长的情况下，没有 ZDR，AI 扩散会明显变慢。
