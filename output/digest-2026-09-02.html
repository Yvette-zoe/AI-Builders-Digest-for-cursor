Box CEO Aaron Levie says better open-weight base models plus more mature, commercialized post-training infra open a new play for companies with large proprietary corpora: train your own models instead of only licensing data outward. Frontier general-purpose models will still lead on the widest task set, but he expects far more vertical and domain models. On security, he argues rising AI security events make sophisticated detection and prevention agents critical; frontier models still lead in cyber, but open models are catching up fast.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，更强的开源基座模型，加上更成熟、已商业化的 post-training 基础设施，给拥有大量专有语料的公司打开了新玩法：不只把数据授权给外部训练，也可以合理训练自己的模型，而不必承担与实验室拼研究的成本和复杂度。通用 frontier 模型仍会在最广任务面上占优，但他预期每个垂直与领域会出现远多于今天的模型。安全方面，他说 AI 安全事件增多，检测与防护类 agent 将至关重要；frontier 模型在 cyber 上仍领先，但开源模型正在快速追上。
