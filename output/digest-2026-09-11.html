Box CEO Aaron Levie argues coding agents will be used far more broadly than people expected: new tool categories, software for companies that could not afford it before, cyber defense, life-sciences research, heavy data workflows, and legacy upgrades. Lowering the cost of code means more useful software and higher leverage for engineers, which in his view means needing more engineers, not fewer. Separately, he reconciles high AI capability with slow GDP impact: diffusion takes longer than people think and is hard to measure immediately, because corporate physics (data pipelines, process change, alignment) and real-world latency (customers, permits, drug pipelines) still bind you; many daily AI wins are near-term GDP-neutral. He expects AI diffusion to be the theme of the next decade, with huge opportunity in bridging superintelligence to real workflows.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为 coding agents 的用途会远超此前预期：新工具品类、服务以前买不起软件的公司、网络安全、生命科学研究、复杂数据工作流、以及遗留系统升级。降低代码成本会让软件更有用、工程师杠杆更大，因此他判断需要更多而非更少工程师。另外，他对齐「能力很强」与「GDP 反应慢」：扩散比想象中慢，也难立刻用 GDP 衡量，因为仍受企业物理规律（数据准备、流程改造、对齐）与真实世界时延（客户回复、许可、药物管线）约束；很多日常 AI 收益短期内对 GDP 近乎中性。他预期 AI diffusion 会是未来十年主题，在超级智能与真实工作流之间搭桥有巨大机会。

Every CEO Dan Shipper critiques a well-designed automation-and-jobs report for two assumptions that change the analysis once stressed: that jobs decompose cleanly into tasks, and that human tasks created by automation are always a fraction of automated tasks (i.e. automation always reduces or at best holds human labor constant). He argues automation often creates multiples more human work, and that a job is also a way of seeing and caring about the world (borrowing Wittgenstein and Heidegger), so new tools generate new tasks that task-list models miss.

Every CEO Dan Shipper 批评一份设计精良的自动化与就业报告中两个会显著改写结论的假设：工作可干净地拆成任务；以及自动化创造的人类任务总是自动化任务的一小部分（即自动化总是减少或最多持平人类劳动）。他认为自动化经常创造出数倍的人类工作；而工作也是看待与关心世界的方式（借用 Wittgenstein 与 Heidegger），工具与情境变化会生成任务清单模型抓不住的新任务。
