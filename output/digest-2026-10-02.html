AI & I by Every: How Sam Altman Uses Dots to Take Back His Time

The Takeaway: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's Dot (he is currently trying names like Dottie) is the first tool that gave him back control of his mornings: it judges what is urgent, handles the rest, and restores the deep-work window he used to lose the moment he checked overnight fires.

要点： OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 的 Dot（他暂时在用 Dottie 这类名字）是第一个让他重新掌控早晨的工具：由它判断什么紧急、其余代为处理，从而找回他一旦查看过夜火灾就会丢掉的深度工作时段。

At Dev Day, Altman tells Every CEO Dan Shipper that coding models never let developers do more, yet the bar has never been higher, because "people just want so much more stuff." He was never as doomy on jobs as others, but still surprised by how much latent demand exists and how centered people remain. Low-creativity work nobody cares who does will automate; everything else will keep inventing new demand. The deeper reason AI stays human-centered, in his view: "we don't care that much about what the machines do... we're obsessed with other people."

在 Dev Day，Altman 对 Every CEO Dan Shipper 说：coding model 让开发者能干的事从未更多，门槛也从未更高，因为 “people just want so much more stuff”。他本人从没有别人那么悲观，但仍惊讶于世界里潜伏需求之大、以及人始终站在一切中心。低创造力、没人在乎是谁做的工作会被自动化；其余领域会不断长出新需求。AI 仍以人为中心的更深原因，在他看来是：“we don't care that much about what the machines do... we're obsessed with other people.”

He frames the preferred future as a renaissance (people-centered) rather than an industrial revolution that turns people into specialized cogs. Dots are his concrete example: before, checking the phone for overnight disasters polluted the creative morning; now he trusts the Dot's judgment. During the keynote, a teammate's Dot warned five minutes before a live demo that a set change would break it. Separately, after Altman gave up searching Slack with Codex for something he was sure he had seen, his Dot kept looking overnight, realized it was a screenshot not text, and found it.

他把更想要的未来框成以人为主的文艺复兴，而不是把人变成专用齿轮的工业革命。Dots 是他的具体例子：以前为了防过夜灾难一查手机，早晨创造力就被污染；现在他信任 Dot 的判断。Keynote 期间，同事的 Dot 在 live demo 前五分钟提醒布景变化可能导致失败。另一次，他用 Codex 搜 Slack 找不到确信见过的内容后放弃了，Dot 却连夜继续找，发现那是截图不是文字，最后翻出来了。

On platforms, he says OpenAI still has not figured out the App Store equivalent, but has figured out how to help millions build. He wants apps in ChatGPT, a marketplace, and the ability to take your ChatGPT subscription everywhere so builders are not bankrupted by inference costs. Ultrafast, which he uses for all his prompting, changed his iterative thinking loop the way Bret Victor argued creators need an immediate connection to what they create. Historically OpenAI democratized the price of IQ; now speed is the next vector to democratize, with their own chip as a contributor, while a real frontier of intelligence or speed will always sit at a higher price.

在平台上，他说 OpenAI 还没搞出 App Store 的等价物，但已经知道如何帮数百万人构建。他想要 ChatGPT 内的 apps、marketplace，以及把 ChatGPT 订阅带到任何地方，让应用层不被 inference 成本拖垮。他自己全部用 Ultrafast 做 prompting，反馈速度改变了迭代思考回路，接近 Bret Victor 说的创作者应对创作物有即时连接。历史上 OpenAI 把 IQ 的价格民主化了；现在速度是下一条要民主化的向量，自研芯片会是贡献之一，但智能或速度的真正 frontier 会始终落在更高价位。
