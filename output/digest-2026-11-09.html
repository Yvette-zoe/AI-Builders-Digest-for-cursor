Aaron Levie (CEO, Box)

Box CEO Aaron Levie says teams are already seeing productivity gains an order of magnitude or two above pre-AI output when they combine the right harness, deep domain intent, often a newer codebase, fluency with multiple agents, and low-friction test/deploy. The gap is not converging: prior depth plus willingness to work differently compounds as models take on larger, harder work, first in software engineering and next in computer-use-heavy domains like science, AEC, finance, and cyber.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为：在正确 harness、对要做什么有深刻领域理解、通常较新的代码库、能同时调度多个 agent，以及低摩擦的测试与部署之下，已经能看到比 AI 之前高一到两个数量级的产出。这个差距不会收敛：你过去积累的深度，加上现在愿意换工作方式，会随着模型能承接更大、更难的任务而复利放大；眼下在软件工程最明显，但科学、AEC、金融、网络安全等重度 computer-use 领域也会跟上。

Separately he argues long-running agents make inference less latency-sensitive, so work can run in remote "AI factories," process encrypted data ephemerally, and return high-value results even with an extra second of delay. That flexibility expands where AI data centers can be deployed, and becomes both an economic opportunity and a loss for places that opt out.

另一边他说，长跑 agent 让推理对时延没那么敏感：任务可以丢到远程 “AI factories”，以加密方式短暂处理数据后丢弃，即便多等一秒，只要任务经济价值够高也划算。这扩大了 AI 数据中心的选址自由度，对愿意承接的国家/地区是机会，对放弃的则是损失。

Agentic finance is the clearest retail AI wedge so far. Robinhood started with a trading MCP, then announced Robinhood Agents at Hood Summit: a Claude Code / Codex-like harness inside the brokerage, with skills, loops, and an agent app store (for example satellite data to count cars in retailer parking lots).
