Latent Space / Building Visual Intelligence — Andreas Blattmann & Dustin Podell, Black Forest Labs

The Takeaway: Black Forest Labs was never meant to be "just" an image lab. Flux.1 was the commercial kickstart; the real thesis is video modeling as the path to understanding how the world works, then pushing that stack into robotics.

一句话： Black Forest Labs 从来就不该只是一家图像实验室。Flux.1 是商业冷启动；真正的 thesis 是用 video modeling 理解世界如何运转，再把同一套能力推进到 robotics。

Black Forest Labs co-founder Andreas Blattmann (Andi; co-inventor of latent diffusion) and research lead Dustin Podell (SDXL, Control LoRA, SD3 post-training, Flux Ultra / Flux.1 / Flux Context) tell the Stability-to-BFL story as a value-capture correction. At Stability they could build impactful models but "couldn't essentially leverage the value of what the models could be," and founding BFL was the chance to "correct those inefficiencies or mistakes of not being able to actually capture the value of the models themselves." Published numbers in the episode: about $450M raised and $100M revenue (as of the December figure they cite). Stealth started at roughly 13-14 people; a France chalet "founding offsite" kicked off Flux.1 training, and three months later the launch model "blew pretty much everyone away."

Black Forest Labs 联合创始人 Andreas Blattmann（Andi；latent diffusion 共同发明人）与研究负责人 Dustin Podell（SDXL、Control LoRA、SD3 post-training、Flux Ultra / Flux.1 / Flux Context）把从 Stability 到 BFL 的故事讲成一次「抓住模型价值」的纠偏。在 Stability，他们能做出有影响力的模型，却「essentially leverage the value of what the models could be」做不到；创办 BFL 就是去「correct those inefficiencies or mistakes of not being able to actually capture the value of the models themselves」。节目里给出的公开数字：累计融资约 $450M，营收 $100M（他们引用的 12 月披露口径）。Stealth 起步大约 13-14 人；法国木屋「founding offsite」启动 Flux.1 训练，三个月后发布模型「blew pretty much everyone away」。

On product philosophy, Dustin's push behind Flux Context is escaping the generate-and-reroll "slot machine": "I just wanted to get away from the slot machine… iterative editing was much more effective." Character-consistent editing plus distillation made chat-and-change workflows fast enough for normal people, and the host argues Flux Context sits on the path to replacing everyday Canva / Photoshop edits (not the top-end market). Older lore still matters: SDXL's refiner was secretly a second full diffusion model specialized for high-frequency detail after structure was set, an early "different model, different job" split of the noise-to-image trajectory. Looking ahead, they repeat the line they say they already believed at Stability in 2023: "video modeling is actually the central means to understanding how our world basically works," which is why C-series pitches were about video, not staying an image shop, with robotics now in view.

产品哲学上，Dustin 推动 Flux Context 的核心是逃离反复抽卡的「slot machine」：「I just wanted to get away from the slot machine… iterative editing was much more effective。」角色一致编辑加 distillation，让「生成后聊天改」对普通人够快；主持人认为 Flux Context 最终可取代日常 Canva / Photoshop 级编辑（不是高端市场）。更早的 lore 仍有用：SDXL 的 refiner 其实是另一台完整 diffusion model，专攻结构定好后的高频细节，是早期把 noise-to-image 轨迹拆成「different model, different job」的做法。往前看，他们重申自 2023 年还在 Stability 时就相信的判断：「video modeling is actually the central means to understanding how our world basically works」，所以 C 轮叙事是 video 而非停留在图像，并已把 robotics 放进视野。
