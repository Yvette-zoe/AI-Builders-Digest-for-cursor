AI & I by Every / Notion’s Founder on the One Thing Your AI Is Missing


The Takeaway: Vibe coding gives everyone clay; what still wins is Lego: opinionated structure, shared context, and tools that help humans keep understanding instead of surrendering it to agents.


一句话： Vibe coding 给了每个人黏土；真正赢的仍是乐高：带观点的结构、可共享的 context，以及帮人类保住理解力、而不是把理解拱手让给 agents 的工具。


Notion CEO and founder Ivan Zhao (ASR also hears "Zao" once) tells Dan Shipper that vibe coding's spirit is the same 60s/70s medium-computing dream Notion started from: software should be as readable and writable as language, not frozen into apps made by a few for users who cannot rewrite them. Claude Code-era agents finally revive that "hippie generation" vision. But "just because you can doesn't mean you should": calendars, chairs, and enterprise security are categories where most people want a crafted default, not a personal fork. Notion still ships that craft lane (he flags a from-scratch native mobile Calendar as one of the better-crafted Notion pieces).


Notion CEO 兼创始人 Ivan Zhao（ASR 有一处听成 Zao）对 Dan Shipper 说，vibe coding 的精神就是 Notion 起步时那套 60/70 年代 medium-computing 梦想：软件应像语言一样可读写，而不是冻成少数制作者给不能改写的用户用的 app。Claude Code 时代的 agents 终于让「嬉皮士一代」愿景复活。但「能做不代表该做」：日历、椅子、企业安全这类，多数人要的是打磨好的默认方案，不是人手一份 fork。Notion 仍做这条 craft 线（他点名从零重做的原生移动端 Calendar，是 Notion 产品里打磨得更好的之一）。


The missing piece is not more prompts. Zhao notices himself chatting with models more than reading, and it can feel TikTok-gross after enough minutes. His hard line: "I don't think we should surrender our understanding. Definitely not without putting up a fight." Agents can do a lot, but they cannot own your understanding; toolmakers should build better abstractions and visualizations so teams still know what the system is doing. Concrete Notion practice: after everyone talked to their own coding-agent black box via GitHub merges, the team put work on a Notion Kanban again, because a shared board is a better metaphor for orchestrating many agents than private chats.


缺的不是更多 prompt。Zhao 发现自己更爱跟模型聊而不是读书，聊久了会有点像刷 TikTok 后的恶心感。他的硬线是：「I don't think we should surrender our understanding. Definitely not without putting up a fight.」Agents 能干很多事，但不能替你拥有理解；工具作者该做更好的抽象与可视化，让团队仍知道系统在干什么。Notion 的具体做法：大家各自对着 coding-agent 黑盒、再用 GitHub merge 之后，团队又把工作拉回 Notion Kanban，因为共享看板比私聊更适合编排一群 agents。


On structure vs tokens, he prefers Lego over clay: vibe coding hands you fine-grained mud that falls apart without maker taste; the industry is converging on vibe-coding Legos (permissions, sync, collaboration handled) so you still express business logic on solid pieces. Notion's emerging answer includes agent-readable pattern libraries and databases still gated by Notion permissions, plus a near-term goal: an out-of-the-box context/know-how layer a ~50-person company can onboard through an agent. In a world that may spend hundreds of billions of dollars on tokens, he frames content/know-how as the compressed opposite of tokens, and assumes the next billion knowledge workers online will mostly be agents, while still insisting human+agent camaraderie on a shared canvas matters more than everyone disappearing into a personal chat box.


在结构 vs tokens 上，他更站乐高而不是黏土：vibe coding 给你细粒度的泥，没有 maker 品味就塌；行业正在收敛到 vibe-coding 乐高（权限、同步、协作先打好），让你仍在稳固积木上表达业务逻辑。Notion 的答案包括 agents 可读的 pattern libraries 与仍受 Notion 权限守护的 databases，以及近几季度目标：给约 50 人公司开箱即用的 context/know-how 层，用 agent 引导 onboard。在一个可能有数千亿美元 token 支出的世界里，他把 content/know-how 说成 tokens 的压缩对立面；并假设下一批上线的十亿知识工作者里绝大多数会是 agents，但仍坚持人+agent 在共享画布上的协作，好过所有人消失进各自的聊天框。
