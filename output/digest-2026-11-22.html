Aaron Levie (CEO, Box)


Box CEO Aaron Levie says model capability jumped enough that instead of telling agents precisely what to do, it is better to use them on problems you cannot easily define yourself. A year ago you had to spell out how to design and build a product idea or success was almost impossible; now agents can often invent a better implementation by searching a larger solution space than you can hold. He expects this across knowledge work (finance, legal, life sciences, cyber): less explicit how-to, more goals plus freedom to pursue them.


Box CEO Aaron Levie 说模型能力已跃迁到：与其精确指挥 agents 怎么做，不如把它们用在你自己也很难说清的问题上。一年前你必须把产品设计与实现路径讲死，否则几乎没成功率；现在 agents 往往能在更大解空间里连点，提出比你预想更好的实现。他预计这会席卷各类知识工作（金融、法律、生命科学、安全）：少给 how-to，多给目标与足够自由度。

Latent Space / Scaling Linear’s GTM to $100M ARR — Cristina Cordova, COO


**The Takeaway:** Manual issue tracking is what dies in the agent era, not the issue unit itself; purpose-built systems that agents can read beat infinitely customizable tools, and Linear's GTM bet is making the product inevitable without forward-deployed engineering theater.


**一句话：** Agent 时代死掉的是人工 issue tracking，不是 issue 这个单元本身；agents 读得懂的 purpose-built 系统胜过无限可定制工具，而 Linear 的 GTM 赌注是让产品变得 inevitable，而不是靠 forward-deployed engineering 演戏。


Linear COO Cristina Cordova (feed title; transcript ASR also "Christina") joined about 3.5 years ago after Notion platform/API/self-serve growth and 7.5 years at Stripe partnerships and product. She cautions founders against hiring a COO too early: often you need sellers first, and you hire someone like her when founders know design/engineering excellence but cannot yet judge what "great" looks like in sales, marketing, or ops. Her trial project was the real job: a 6-12 month GTM plan aimed at making Linear inevitable (you are already on it, or you are planning the move). Since then the company has at least 10Xed; she says they are ~170 people, still flat, with founders plus one business-side C-suite title.


Linear COO Cristina Cordova（feed 标题；transcript ASR 也听成 Christina）约 3.5 年前加入，此前在 Notion 做平台/API/自助增长，并在 Stripe 做了 7.5 年合作与产品。她常劝创始人别过早招 COO：很多时候先要销售，只有当创始人知道设计/工程的「优秀」长什么样、却还不会判断销售/市场/运营的「优秀」时，才适合招她这类人。试用期项目就是真工作：一份 6-12 个月 GTM 计划，核心是让 Linear 变得 inevitable（你已经在用，或你在计划迁过去）。此后公司至少 10X；她说大约 170 人、仍偏扁平，C-suite 基本是创始人加她这一位业务侧头衔。


On Karri's "issue tracking is dead" blog post (host ASR "Kari"), Cordova's line is that Linear is still small enough not to kill itself the way Google struggles to put an AI box atop search without nuking ads: lean into the new era rather than clutch the old workflow. "Yes, the unit of the issue still exists, but we probably shouldn't have a world where people are like manually tracking work. So like that's what's dead." Customers mostly agreed and then asked what they were buying next.


谈到 Karri「issue tracking is dead」博客（host ASR 为 Kari），Cordova 的说法是：Linear 还没大到会「弄死自己」，不像 Google 很难在搜索顶上塞 AI 框却不伤广告；要投入新时代，而不是死抱旧工作流。「issue 这个单元还在，但我们大概不该活在人人手工 track 工作的世界里。死掉的是这个。」客户大多表示同意，然后追问接下来到底在买什么。


GTM specifics: historically PLG and seat-based (she cites ~40,000 paying customers; seat era through about June), with logos like Vercel and Ramp that never talked to a human. Enterprise motion needed contracts, pilots, and internal selling help for champions vs IT/procurement. They hire AEs who already sold technical products to discerning builders, add solutions engineers later, refuse SDRs and forward-deployed engineering, and keep support in-house. Philosophy: "we believe Linear should be easy enough for someone to integrate." Purpose-built beats infinitely customizable admin empires, and that consistency makes Linear "far easier for agents to understand" than tools where "bug" means different things per team. On the AI-budget gold rush that can mint $100M ARR via a few $30-40M deals, she contrasts Linear's diversified seat base: harder to jump 0→100 overnight, but also less concentrated. Long trusted customers sometimes sign multi-year deals; many AI buyers still prefer pilots/one-year while the stack churns. Investor lesson: "closed mouths don't get fed."


GTM 细节：历史上偏 PLG 与按席位（她提到约 4 万付费客户；席位模式大约持续到那年 6 月），Vercel、Ramp 这类客户甚至从未与人交谈。企业销售需要合同、试点，并帮 champion 对 IT/采购做内部销售。他们招已经向挑剔 builder 卖过技术产品的 AE，稍后才加 solutions engineer，不做 SDR、不做 forward-deployed engineering，客服也坚持自建。哲学是：「我们相信 Linear 应该简单到客户自己就能集成。」Purpose-built 胜过无限可定制的 admin 帝国，而这种一致性让 agents「更容易读懂」Linear，不像有些工具里「bug」标签每个团队含义都不同。谈到靠几笔 $30-40M 单子冲到 $100M ARR 的 AI 预算淘金潮，她对比 Linear 更分散的席位底座：更难一夜 0→100，也不那么集中。受信任客户有时签多年单；许多 AI 买家在栈还在剧变时仍偏试点/一年期。对投资人的教训：「closed mouths don't get fed」（不开口就没人喂你）。
