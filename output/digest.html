AI educator Peter Yang lays out armchair takes on the personal agent race. He puts Muse ahead as Meta's next big homegrown app after Facebook (bigger than Threads): the standalone Muse app already feels more natural than stuffing an agent into iMessage. ChatGPT still leads on distribution (1B+ users) and often wins feature-by-feature on models, computer use, and voice, but one product for both work and personal (plus enterprise vs consumer UX) is hard; he even started using Grok Bot for work because the Work/Codex split is confusing. Grok Bot, in his bull case, is a few features from becoming multiplayer agentic Slack for bots and humans. Google is the dark horse because agents live off Gmail/Calendar/app data, yet Spark is still a secondary Gemini tab and needs to become the primary, Muse-competitive experience. Nobody has multiplayer figured out yet (spouse on Muse, coworkers in ChatGPT/Grok threads). His stack today: Muse personal, Grok Bot for cloud tasks, Claude for specific cases, ChatGPT for everything else; he also flags Apple/Siri as blocked by annual release cycles and slow public iteration. Advice: design personal skills and files so they port across harnesses, or you will spend half your time migrating.

No Priors: Coinbase’s Everything Exchange: Agentic Finance, Stablecoins, and Tokenization with CEO Brian Armstrong


The Takeaway: Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong's bet is blunt: do not leave the AIs unbanked, because agent ecommerce is already mostly sub-30¢ micropayments that cards cannot price, and the agentic economy will outgrow the human one.


要点： Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong 的判断很直白：别让 AI 变成 unbanked。agent 电商多数已是低于 30¢ 的小额支付，传统卡组织定不了价；agentic economy 迟早会超过人类经济。


Armstrong frames Coinbase around three towers. Everything Exchange pulls stocks, commodities, crypto, derivatives, perps, and prediction markets into one liquid, cross-margined venue as assets come on-chain and regulation clears more countries. Stablecoin payments keep growing even when Bitcoin is down: money moves "at the speed of information," roughly under one second and under 1¢ US anywhere. Agentic Finance (AIFi) is the third: an in-product AI advisor that democratizes portfolio construction, DCA, tax-loss harvesting, and cash yield via conversation; plus wallets for the agents themselves.


Armstrong 把 Coinbase 拆成三根柱子。Everything Exchange 把股票、大宗商品、crypto、衍生品、永续与预测市场收进同一套高流动性、可 cross margin 的场所，资产上链与监管清晰化正在打开更多国家。Stablecoin payments 在比特币下跌时仍在增长：资金按“信息的速度”流动，大约一秒内、不到 1¢ 美元可全球到账。第三根是 Agentic Finance（AIFi）：产品内 AI advisor 用对话普及组合构建、定投、tax-loss harvesting 与现金收益；同时给 agent 本身开钱包。


The micropayment math drives the stack. Card rails often charge ~30¢ flat plus a percentage, useless for a 1¢ agent payment; about 76% of agent ecommerce transactions Coinbase sees are under 30¢. Agents get self-custodial crypto accounts via a pasteable prompt, no KYC and no government ID required. Spend flows over protocols like X402 (incubated at Coinbase, now in the Linux Foundation with Google, Cloudflare, AWS and others), with edgentic.market showing live payment stats. Coinbase Business lets merchants take stablecoins from humans and agents in one box. Tokenization extends the same pattern: real 1:1 custody-backed tokenized stocks (ex-US for now) after stablecoins proved the template for 4B people without US brokerage access.


小额支付的账决定了技术栈。卡组织常见约 30¢ 固定费加百分比，对 1¢ 的 agent 支付根本不合理；Coinbase 看到的 agent 电商交易约 76% 低于 30¢。agent 用一条可粘贴的 prompt 就能开出自托管 crypto 账户，无需 KYC，也没有政府证件。支出走 X402 这类协议（Coinbase 孵化，已进入 Linux Foundation，Google、Cloudflare、AWS 等参与），edgentic.market 展示实时支付统计。Coinbase Business 让商户一套接入同时收人类与 agent 的 stablecoin。tokenization 是同一模板的延伸：在 stablecoin 证明模式之后，推出真正 1:1 托管背书的 tokenized stocks（目前主要在美国以外），面向约 40 亿没有美股券商账户的人群。
