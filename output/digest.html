The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck: When AI Improves Itself | Richard Socher (Recursive)

The Takeaway: Recursive founder Richard Socher bets that anything you can simulate, AI will solve, and the path to scientific breakthroughs is recursive self-improvement: make AI excellent at inventing better AI first, then point that loop at biology and the rest of science.

要点： Recursive founder Richard Socher 押注：凡是能模拟的，AI 最终都能解决；通往科学突破的路径是 recursive self-improvement：先让 AI 擅长发明更好的 AI，再把这个环指向 biology 与其他科学。

Socher, one of the most-cited AI researchers and author of the forthcoming book The Eureka Machine (out around September 22), raised roughly $650M+ for Recursive to industrialize that loop. His diagnosis of slowing discovery: fields splintered into a "labyrinth of knowledge" across tens of thousands of journals, so cross-domain Renaissance-style insight is nearly impossible by hand. AI, he argues, is to biology what calculus was to physics: the language that weaves tiny understood pieces (neurons, proteins, cells) back into systems we can actually explore.

Socher 是被引用最多的 AI 研究者之一，新书 The Eureka Machine（约 9 月 22 日上市），并为 Recursive 筹集约 $650M+ 来工业化这条环路。他对科学放缓的诊断是：知识已碎成 “labyrinth of knowledge”，上万期刊把领域切得太细，靠人手做跨学科洞见几乎不可能。他认为 AI 之于 biology，就像 calculus 之于 physics：能把神经元、蛋白质、细胞这些已理解的小块重新织成可探索的系统。

A concrete claim: next-token prediction is not "just chat." Train on enough sequences and the model absorbs geography, protein contact structure, and other domain structure the same way. Domains with simulators and verifiers (games, math, programming) fall first; natural sciences lag until we collect far more perturbation data and can approximate a virtual cell. Recursive's sequencing is blunt: AI-for-AI until the system has the equivalent of tens of thousands of PhDs, then physics/chemistry/biology. Hallucination, in exploration mode, can be a feature for inventing novel proteins; for facts, ground the model in search.

一个具体论点：next-token prediction 不只是聊天。序列够多时，模型会像吸收地理关系一样吸收蛋白质接触结构等 domain 结构。有模拟器与 verifier 的领域（游戏、数学、编程）会先被攻克；自然科学要等更多扰动实验数据、逼近 virtual cell。Recursive 的顺序很直白：先做 AI-for-AI，直到系统相当于数万 PhD，再进物理/化学/生物。探索新蛋白时，hallucination 可以是 feature；问事实时则要用搜索 grounding。

The Eureka Machine's four pillars are human knowledge / LMs, measurements, simulation, and robotic data collection, topped by an agent swarm of scientists. Company proof points already include AI research artifacts that beat months or years of human effort on narrow problems, plus new CUDA kernels useful for faster inference (with positive feedback against NVIDIA's SOLEXEC-style benches). Most of the raise is going into compute (on the order of a $410M Amazon deal). Memorable line: "Anything you can simulate, AI will solve."
