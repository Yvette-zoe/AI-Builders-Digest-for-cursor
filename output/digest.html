Cursor designer Ryo Lu (prev Notion, Stripe) warns of "convergence to the mean": when everyone copies what works and lets feedback author the work, products get polished, interchangeable, and mid. AI makes that loop frictionless by drawing from more references than a lifetime of looking, but access to every perspective is not the same as having one. He wants makers to approach software more like artists: develop a point of view through making, noticing, and living, then ask what someone should feel, understand, or do, rather than borrowing a competitor's roadmap.

Cursor designer Ryo Lu（前 Notion、Stripe）警告 “convergence to the mean”：当人人复制有效做法、让反馈反过来主导创作，产品会变得精致、可互换、平庸。AI 让这个回路几乎无摩擦，因为它能从毕生都看不完的参考里取材，但接触到所有视角不等于自己拥有一个视角。他希望做东西的人更像艺术家对待媒介：在制作、察觉与生活里长出观点，再问别人该感受到、理解或做到什么，而不是从竞品 roadmap 借答案。
