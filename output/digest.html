Ep 96: Nebius CTO on Racing the Hyperscalers Before AWS Wakes Up (Unsupervised Learning)

The Takeaway: Nebius is betting it can grow into a true AI Cloud peer of AWS and Google Cloud while hyperscalers still treat GPU cloud as a relatively small, infrastructure-awkward business - because the real moat is software that can sell the same platform retail (one GPU for half an hour) and wholesale (tens of thousands of GPUs in one cluster).

The Takeaway： Nebius 的赌注是：在 hyperscaler 仍把 GPU 云当成相对较小、且要改造旧基础设施才划算的业务时，先长成能与 AWS、Google Cloud 同台竞争的 AI Cloud；真正护城河是同一套软件平台既能零售（半小时一张 GPU），也能批发（一个互联集群里上万张 GPU）。

On Unsupervised Learning, Nebius CTO Danila (host ASR also hears "Neela"; no surname in the feed title) refuses the "neo cloud" label - the umbrella stretched from about five to seven companies at the start to "three to 500" - and says Nebius is past competing in that bucket: "We are the AI Cloud." Revenue is maybe 1% of AWS, but demand over the next three years could rise tenfold or hundredfold, and much of today's giant capacity (Colossus-class builds, lab-owned clusters) is captive to internal product needs rather than a rented mid-market. The bet is an independent provider for mid-tier players who want to become large companies, while Google, Meta, and Microsoft keep juggling internal research/runtime against what they can rent out.

在 Unsupervised Learning 上，Nebius CTO Danila（主持人 ASR 有一处听成 Neela；feed 标题未给姓氏）拒绝 "neo cloud" 标签 - 这个筐从起步时大约五到七家，已被撑到「三到五百」 - 并说 Nebius 已不在那个池子里竞争：「We are the AI Cloud。」收入或许只有 AWS 的 1%，但未来三年需求可能再涨十倍乃至百倍；而今天大量巨型产能（Colossus 级自建、实验室自用集群）服务的是内部产品，不是可租的中腰部市场。赌注是做一个不绑定大厂内部消耗的独立供给方，服务那些想长成大公司的 mid-tier；与此同时 Google、Meta、Microsoft 还在内部 research/runtime 与对外出租之间撕扯。

Origin story is separation from Yandex into a clean 2024 company, keeping a hardware ops core that had run hundreds of thousands to millions of servers, then rewriting the software platform from scratch and hiring hard so that by late 2024 much of software eng was no longer ex-Yandex. The staffing tell: hardware buildout was roughly 40-50 people while software was already about 200 - "software first," not managed bare metal or a patchwork of third-party services. Bringing up new NVIDIA generations is iterative and messy (schematics change, lab samples diverge, isolation shifts from servers to racks on GB300-class designs), and storage was the early underestimation: customers now routinely pull on the order of 500-700 GB/s from a shared volume across thousands of GPUs, after a prior career spent talking about GB/s-class network storage as the high end.

起源是与 Yandex 切割后的 2024 新公司，保留曾运维数十万到百万级服务器的硬件核心，同时把软件平台推倒重写并猛招人，以至于 2024 年底软件工程里已有很大比例不再是前 Yandex。编制很能说明问题：硬件建设大约 40-50 人，软件已到约 200 - 「software first」，不是 managed bare metal，也不是第三方服务拼盘。跟进新一代 NVIDIA 是反复迭代的脏活（图纸改、样品跑偏、GB300 类架构的隔离从 server 升到 rack），而早期低估的是存储：客户现在常从挂在数千 GPU 上的共享卷打出约 500-700 GB/s 吞吐，而他上一份基础设施生涯里，GB/s 级网络存储一度已是高端叙事。

On inference he is blunt: "as an industry, we didn't optimize shit," vanilla open-weight serving is often underwhelming versus frontier APIs, and about 95% of Nebius customers do not run vanilla open-source models - they fine-tune and specialize, which opens pruning/compression and kernel work for scoped domains where predictability and wall-clock time matter more than raw model size. Disaggregated prefill/decode exists in production for demanding customers as a white-glove setup, not a universal go-to. Hyperscalers are not asleep on big deals or pricing, but chip shortage plus the cost of rewiring networking/storage stacks keeps GPU cloud a portfolio edge case until supply loosens in a few years - "the question is whether the novel players... will be fast enough to grow and represent significant competition when they get there." The single bottleneck across GPU/CPU/NVMe/power/transformer lead times is "your ability to execute."

谈到 inference 他很直接：「as an industry, we didn't optimize shit」；原样跑开源权重往往打不过 frontier API；Nebius 约 95% 客户也不跑 vanilla 开源模型，而是 fine-tune / 专项化，这为限定域里的 pruning、compression 与 kernel 优化打开空间 - 那里结果可预期与墙钟时间比模型尺寸更重要。拆分 prefill/decode 已在高要求客户生产里以白手套方式存在，但还不是普适方案。Hyperscaler 在大单与定价上并未睡着，只是芯片短缺，加上重做网络/存储栈的成本，让 GPU 云在供给缓和前仍像组合里的边缘业务 - 「问题是新玩家是否够快，能在他们认真进来时构成实质性竞争。」横跨 GPU/CPU/NVMe/电力/变压器交期，唯一瓶颈是「your ability to execute」。
