No Priors — Powering AI-Driven Drug Discovery with President of Isomorphic Labs Max Jaderberg

The Takeaway: the bottleneck in medicine is not more wet-lab throughput; it is getting predictive, experimental-accuracy models so you enter the real world to validate, not to learn by expensive trial and error.

核心结论：制药瓶颈不是把湿实验吞吐再拉高一点，而是把预测模型做到接近 experimental accuracy，让你进入真实世界是为了验证，而不是用昂贵试错去学习。

Isomorphic Labs president and founding-team member Max Jaderberg (host ASR also hears "Jadberg"; DeepMind alum; PhD in computer vision around the AlexNet turn; company sold into DeepMind in 2014; host ASR recalls him asking Jan Lakun about convnets) frames Isomorphic's bet since ~2021: AlphaFold showed deep learning can generalize in a meaningful biology space, so Alphabet-backed Isomorphic exists to change how we think about human health and "solving all disease," with its own culture and cross-disciplinary pace.

Isomorphic Labs 总裁兼创始团队成员 Max Jaderberg（主持人 ASR 也听成 “Jadberg”；DeepMind 出身；约在 AlexNet 转折期做计算机视觉博士；2014 年公司卖入 DeepMind；主持人 ASR 提到他曾向 Jan Lakun 请教卷积网络）把约 2021 年起的赌注说清楚：AlphaFold 证明深度学习能在有意义的生物学空间泛化，所以 Alphabet 支持的 Isomorphic 要改变我们对人类健康与 “solving all disease” 的想象，并配上自己的文化与跨学科节奏。

Drug discovery stays stubbornly empirical while papers and modalities explode and approvals stay flat, costs climb toward ~$1.5B, and timelines stretch toward ~15 years. Low-hanging medicines found by trial-and-error get picked; what remains is combinatorially huge biology plus molecule design that screening cannot reach. Culture and missing R&D economies of scale matter too: each program is often a bespoke frontier project with little transferable learning. AI platforms that absorb disease-biology and molecule-design lessons across programs are how compounding returns become possible.

在论文与 modality 爆炸、获批数却基本持平、成本冲向约 $1.5B、周期拉到约 15 年时，药物发现仍顽固地经验主义。试错能摘到的低挂果实被摘完后，剩下的是组合爆炸的生物学与分子设计，筛选够不着。文化与缺失的研发规模经济也关键：每个项目常是定制前沿科学，可迁移学习很少。能跨项目吸收疾病生物学与分子设计经验的 AI 平台，才可能让收益复利。

Hiring is mashup by design: there are almost no ready-made "AI-for-drug-discovery" experts on Isomorphic's stack, so they hire strong core AI people (even without life-science background) plus strong biologists/chemists (even without AI), then force the culture that grows cross-disciplinary experts inside. For core modeling pods, ratios like ~5 AI people to 1 domain expert resonate. North star: bring discovery onto a computer. Automated labs help shrink real-world iteration, but physics still waits on synthesis and cell culture; the dotted line on internal leaderboards is experimental accuracy. "I'd be really disappointed if we fast forward even five years' time. Like, the predictive understanding of disease biology has not step changed in, like, a massive way."

招聘就是刻意 mashup：几乎没有现成、且用他们这套模型的 “AI 制药专家”，所以招强核心 AI（可以没有生命科学背景）和强生物/化学（可以没有 AI），再逼出内部长出的跨学科专家。核心建模小组里，约 5 个 AI 对 1 个领域专家的比例很共鸣。北极星：把发现搬到计算机上。自动化实验室有助于压缩真实世界迭代，但物理仍要等合成与细胞培养；内部排行榜的虚线目标是 experimental accuracy。“I'd be really disappointed if we fast forward even five years' time. Like, the predictive understanding of disease biology has not step changed in, like, a massive way.”

Public and pharma passive datasets are often biased byproducts; Isomorphic generates a lot of its own data and reports strong data scaling laws. Animal tox gold standards are only ~30% predictive of human clinical tox: "if one of my AI scientists or engineers came up to me and said, hey, we've got this amazing model. It's 30% predictive. You're like, okay. Yeah. Maybe do a bit better than that." The opportunity is in vitro endpoints + predictive models + organoids / organs-on-a-chip aimed at human clinical tox directly. They keep the drug-design engine in-house (no selling tokens / licensing the model), run owned pipeline programs (focus oncology, immunology/inflammation; models themselves disease-/modality-agnostic), and partner with pharma on decade-hard problems. Better-engineered, less-toxic molecules also unlock multi-target cocktails that today's drug-drug interactions block. Patient safety stays central, but risk appetite differs by indication; over time statistically safer AI-designed molecules should reshape how regulation and patients meet new drugs. Clinical timing stays deliberately soft: exciting medicines in the works, clinical development team building up, no hard date.

公开与药企被动数据集常是有偏副产品；Isomorphic 大量自产数据，并称看到很强的 data scaling laws。动物毒理金标准对人体临床毒理大约只有 30% 预测力：“if one of my AI scientists or engineers came up to me and said, hey, we've got this amazing model. It's 30% predictive. You're like, okay. Yeah. Maybe do a bit better than that.” 机会在体外终点 + 预测模型 + organoids / organs-on-a-chip，直接对准人体临床毒理。药物设计引擎留在内部（不卖 tokens / 不授权模型），自有管线聚焦肿瘤与免疫/炎症（模型本身疾病与 modality 无关），并与药企搭档十年级难题。工程得更好、毒性更低的分子，也才能打开今天因 drug-drug interaction 做不成的多靶点 cocktail。患者安全仍居中，但风险偏好随适应症不同；长期看，统计上更安全的 AI 设计分子应改写监管与患者相遇新药的方式。临床时间表故意说软：有很兴奋的药物在推进，临床开发团队在建，没有硬日期。
