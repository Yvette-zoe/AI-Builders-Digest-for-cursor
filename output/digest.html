Peter Yang (AI tutorials / creator)

Creator Peter Yang posts his top five takeaways from Nate Parrott on getting Claude to design without AI slop: (1) give context upfront (screenshots, liked pages, past work; for an existing product, have Claude rebuild real screens from code); (2) research familiar UX patterns for hard layouts; (3) make Claude aware of its design biases (serif, off-white backgrounds, card-edge bars) in design.md; (4) start with wireframes before fonts and colors; (5) ask for multiple options, then remix the parts you like. Episode: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=604T-74s3KE Writeup: https://creatoreconomy.so/p/how-to-fix-ai-slop-with-claude-design-nate-parrott

Creator Peter Yang 总结 Nate Parrott 关于让 Claude 设计时避开 AI slop 的五条： (1) 先给足 context（截图、喜欢的页面、过往作品；已有产品就让 Claude 从代码重建真实界面）； (2) 难布局先研究熟悉产品的 UX 模式； (3) 在 design.md 里点破模型偏爱（serif、米白底、卡片边缘横条）； (4) 先画 wireframe，再抠字体颜色； (5) 要多方案，再混搭喜欢的部分。节目：https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=604T-74s3KE 长文：https://creatoreconomy.so/p/how-to-fix-ai-slop-with-claude-design-nate-parrott
